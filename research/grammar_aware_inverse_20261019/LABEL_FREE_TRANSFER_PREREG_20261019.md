# GAI LF-T1: prospective label-free cross-quire transfer test (2026-10-19)

## Status and nonclaim
Pilot preregistration BEFORE scoring: investigate observable transferable word-cooccurrence beyond a grammar/within-page copy predictor. This is **not** blind inverse qualification, lexical recovery, or semantic identification. Q1B and IDENT-GATE failures remain controlling. Exact observational source/production twins remain non-identifiable; even a positive outcome here could be repeated copying across quires.

## Corpus (read-only)
`public.vms_directional_token_v01` joined by folio to `vms_catalogue.vms_folios`. Filter `section='Herbal-A'`, `illustration='H'`, `currier='A'`, `davis_hand='1'`, non-null token. No Pharma labels, other herbal hand, Q13, Stars, circular, text-only. Folio and paragraph token order given by (folio, integer paragraph number, token pos). Group all folios in the SAME physical **quire** to the same held-out fold. Test seven whole quires A–G (the cohort must be audited). Each folio's first **15** tokens are publicly observable adaptation context, never scored; subsequent tokens are the prospective target. No bifolium or quire crossing between training and test. The observational transcription corpus and source provenance must be audited before confirmatory promotion.

## Frozen pilot models
Target event: token type among training-fold's 96 most frequent exact surface types, plus an OTHER tail event. One category mapping is learned ONLY on training folds. Category tails are not individual-word predictions.
- **M0**: smoothed training empirical unigram and last visible token's first-two-character prefix, plus paragraph position [first / non-first]. Token probability: 0.4 unigram + 0.6 context with 30 pseudocounts to unigram. (Where sparse, this shrinks automatically.) There is no learned plaintext.
- **M1 copy hard negative**: 0.75 M0 + 0.25 within-folio online exponentially decayed cache (decay 0.98) for exact categories and an explicitly normalized one-edit neighbour kernel, smoothed by 12 pseudocounts to the *current* M0. Cache observes only previously visible tokens on test folio (no future, no other held-out pages). Baseline copying capacity is deliberately material.
- **M2 cross-quire association addition**: 0.8 M1 + 0.2 cooccurrence distribution conditioned on up to 10 infrequent early-token anchors (first 15 tokens, ≥4 characters, train-global count 2–35), selected by rarity before the test target is inspected. Per-anchor late-word category frequency trained on other quires' pages only; Dirichlet smoothing by 35 pseudocounts to training global category distribution. If no eligible anchor, M2=M1.
All weights, vocab cap, minimums and seed are discovery-only fixed choices, not calibrated optimum. M2 has greater effective capacity than M1; therefore success does not qualify mechanism M2 in programme terminology. CONNECT/LINE_ENTRY are approximated, not the previously frozen full renderer.

## Hard negative and statistical decision
Randomly permute TRAINING-only *late-token blocks* among folios within the same quire, matching source page late-block lengths approximately by sorting and permuting within successive groups of 4. This preserves entire late production blocks, quire, Currier, Davis hand, section, early word order, and global word counts. Recompute the cross-page cooccurrence feature and its heldout M2 score; hold M0/M1 and every heldout page intact. Use 199 seeded permutations (seed 20261019), score mean log2 gain `mean log2(P_M2/P_M1)` on **previously unseen quires**. Null sd is permutation SD; record effect = observed - null mean, z=effect/null_sd, permutation upper p=(1+#null≥observed)/200. Also report gain by quire and first/second-half quire split. Use absolute bits/token, not AUC.
Prospective pilot threshold: gain ≥+0.02 bits/token, upper p ≤0.05, and positive gain in ≥5 of 7 held-out quires. This is *descriptive observational transfer only* even if passed, not a promotion to source semantics.
A stricter M1 must additionally model cross-quire copying templates, and model capacities must be matched before any content inference. Invariance to transcription layer and physical-bifolium alternatives is not qualified in this pilot.

## Falsification / stop
If improvement ≤0 or does not resolve null (effect / SD <2) -> FAIL or INDETERMINATE: no promotion. If >0 and passing all thresholds -> a reproducible nonlocal association **candidate**, not content. A copy-template generator trained across quires can be observationally equivalent to M2: **do not claim a semantic-vs-production result**. Do not retrofit thresholds, folio subgroup, vocabulary sizes or weights after scores are viewed. Later confirmation requires independently sourced ZLZI/TTLI layers, corpus SHA and truly matched production alternatives.
