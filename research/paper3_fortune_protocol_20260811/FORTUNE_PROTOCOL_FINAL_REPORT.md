# Fortune Protocol Final Report — Paper 3, Draft 13 → Draft 14

## Formal outcome

**INTERNAL TECHNICAL PASS — HUMAN SPECIALIST REVIEW OPEN**

The protocol does **not** endorse the frozen Draft 13 conclusion as written. It forced a material revision.

### Frozen hypothesis
Padua was presented as the densest single candidate production environment.

### Adversarial result
Under a six-domain comparison fixed before final population, **Padua and Pavia/Milan tie at 5/6 strict-window core domains**. The illustrated-balneology access domain remains undemonstrated for both.

### Consequence
The singular Padua ranking failed its own stopping rule. Draft 14 therefore changes both title and conclusion:

> **Northern Italy is the strongest regional working field in the present comparison, with Padua and Pavia/Milan as co-leading institutional nodes and Venice as an ordinary transmission partner for the Paduan network.**

Physical production at a particular city, workshop, or return destination remains open.

## Material corrections forced by the audit

1. Pavia/Milan was upgraded from secondary rival to co-leading node after direct review of its 1426 library scale and pre-1426 astronomical/astrological holdings.
2. Vienna–Klosterneuburg and Bologna were strengthened as controls, blocking any inference from medicine+astronomy alone to Padua.
3. Illustrated *De Balneis* geography was demoted from a Paduan discriminator to an Italian regional prior.
4. The herbal claim was restricted from European illustrated herbals generally to the relevant fantastic-herbal/Veneto cluster, with the French tradition retained as a counter-control.
5. Wellcome MS.213 was removed; MS.327 and MS.683 were disentangled and retained for their correct, separate functions.
6. Foliation/quiremark chronology and the provenance footnote were corrected.
7. Script, *characteres*, cipher, Pisces-first/*More Veneto*, multiple-hand workshop hierarchy, and pharmaceutical semantics were all downgraded where they exceeded the evidence.
8. The Q9/Q10/Q14 computational result remains only a bounded repertoire/recomposition result and carries no geographic localisation.
9. Post-1438 evidence is isolated as persistence evidence.

## Most useful surviving Paduan evidence

The Paduan case remains strong because of the directness of its strict-window controls: scientific compilations copied there in 1401–02, 1424, and 1435, including medically trained copyists/users; established medicine/natural philosophy; Veneto herbal inheritance; routine transalpine participation; and intensive copying networks.

## Why Pavia cannot now be ranked below it under the frozen test

Pavia's Visconti library already held substantial astronomical/astrological material before 1426 and contained 988 manuscripts in that year. Combined with medical teaching, relevant herbal material, trans-European traffic, and court-library copying infrastructure, it reaches the same core coverage.

## Next discriminators

The protocol leaves four high-value tests:
1. systematic comparative palaeography of the VMS primary and secondary hands;
2. direct pre-1438 ownership/transmission of an illustrated balneological picture-cycle at Padua, Pavia, Venice, or a transalpine rival;
3. source-led comparison of complete integrated astronomical/cosmological manuscript programmes rather than isolated folios;
4. archival hand/exemplar matching within the Padua and Pavia/Milan manuscript networks.
