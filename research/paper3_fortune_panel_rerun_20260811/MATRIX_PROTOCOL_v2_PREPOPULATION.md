## Comparison unit

A row is a **documented scholarly-book access system**: a city, court, university, or tightly linked multi-site network within which a compiler could routinely obtain books, training, scribal labour, or specialist contact without an exceptional long-distance acquisition. Multi-site units are allowed only where in-window sources document routine institutional/personnel circulation between the sites. This rule is applied symmetrically.

Primary rows to be tested: Padua–Venice; Pavia–Milan; Bologna; Vienna–Klosterneuburg; Florence; Ferrara; Bavaria/Munich; Upper Rhine/Alsace. A city-only sensitivity will also be reported where aggregation materially affects interpretation.

## Strict window

1404–1438. Evidence after 1438 may be reported only as persistence/context and may not repair a strict-window U cell.

## Domain definitions

D1. **Illustrated medicinal-plant access.** Any illustrated manuscript whose principal or substantial function is medicinal-plant/herbal transmission. No fantastic-herbal, Veneto, or VMS-family assumption is built into the domain.

D2. **Illustrated bathing/process-vessel imagery.** A locally produced/held or routinely accessible illustrated manuscript programme involving therapeutic bathing, water systems, distillation/process vessels, or materially comparable vessel-and-fluid imagery. This domain deliberately does not assume the VMS nymph section is therapeutic bathing.

D3. **Medical-pharmaceutical institutional culture.** Documented university, court, monastic, hospital, guild, or comparable institutional medicine/pharmacy sufficient to support specialist compilation.

D4. **Astronomy/cosmology access and compilation.** Documented teaching plus manuscript ownership/copying, or direct manuscript evidence, for astronomy/astrology/cosmology/computus.

D5. **Cross-regional scholar integration.** Documented routine participation by scholars/students/practitioners from outside the local linguistic/political region, sufficient to provide a normal mechanism for visual/textual transfer.

D6. **Multi-source manuscript-copying infrastructure.** Documented local copying households, stationers, court/library copying, monastic copying, or equivalent infrastructure capable of assembling multi-author working compilations.

## Cell thresholds

D0 = direct strict-window local evidence: at least one dated/localised manuscript, archival record, institutional statute, inventory, or comparably authoritative record that demonstrates the domain within the comparison unit by 1438.

N0 = ordinary strict-window network access: no direct local exemplar is demonstrated, but an authoritative in-window record demonstrates routine institutional circulation/access within the same predeclared comparison unit.

U = not demonstrated after the targeted audit. U means absence of evidence in this audit, not historical absence.

Primary coverage counts D0 and N0 equally as COVERED because the research question is practical access, not ownership. D0/N0 are displayed separately and are not converted into post-hoc numerical weights.

## Secondary corroborators excluded from the score

Cipher availability; More Veneto/Pisces-first; local thermal springs; script impressions; specific merlon/zodiac iconographic parallels; author computational retrieval.

## Stopping rule

No prose tie-breaker may override equal core coverage. If removing D1 and D2 causes non-Italian controls to match the leading Italian systems, the paper must state that the Northern-Italian ranking depends on the pictorial-domain assumptions and is not robust to that sensitivity test.
