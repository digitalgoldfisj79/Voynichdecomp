# D1 strict-window herbal enumeration protocol

Date: 2026-08-11
Target question: does Vienna–Klosterneuburg (or the tightly linked Vienna/Austrian scholarly network) have a securely localised illustrated medicinal-plant manuscript produced there or demonstrably present there by 1438?

## Inclusion
- Date or secure terminus ante quem <= 1438.
- Manuscript contains a substantial medicinal-plant/herbal text or plant-remedy section.
- For D1 illustrated status: manuscript has plant illustrations or an illustrated herbal cycle materially integrated with the medicinal-plant text.
- Production counts if securely localised to the comparison unit.
- Access counts if securely documented in an in-window local institutional/private library or owned/copied by a local user by 1438.

## Exclusion
- Post-1438 production or first-attested access.
- Purely literary botanical texts without medicinal-plant function.
- Decoration only, without plant illustration relevant to the herbal content.
- Current holding place without evidence of medieval production/access there.
- Broad Austrian/German attribution unless the record supports the Vienna–Klosterneuburg network.

## Null rule
A null may be called 'bounded negative evidence' only after systematic search of: Manuscripta.at records for Vienna/Klosterneuburg and Austrian houses in their documented scholarly network; ÖNB/University of Vienna manuscript catalogues; Klosterneuburg catalogue; relevant Handschriftencensus herbal/Macer/Circa instans records; and targeted searches for Macer, Circa instans, Herbarius, Dioscorides, De viribus herbarum, simplicia/simple medicines, Kräuterbuch, herbarium/herbarum, and plant-medical miscellanies.

The same definitions will be applied to Veneto/Padua and Pavia/Milan controls using institutional catalogues and established manuscript censuses. No new domain definition may be introduced after results are known.
