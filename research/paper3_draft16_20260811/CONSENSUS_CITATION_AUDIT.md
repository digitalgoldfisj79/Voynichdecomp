# Consensus citation audit — Draft 16

## Scope

The Consensus connector was used as an independent bibliographic/indexing check on the paper's modern scholarly citations. It is not an appropriate verifier for manuscript shelfmarks, institutional catalogues, archival records, medieval editions, or many humanities monographs; those remain a separate primary/catalogue verification class. Exact-title searches were preferred. A Consensus miss is therefore treated as an indexing result, not evidence that a citation is false.

## Exact-title searches not resolved by Consensus but independently verified

## Citation issues found and corrected

1. **Greshko pagination:** Draft 15 gave `1–37`; current publisher metadata confirms online publication and DOI but not that final pagination. Draft 16 cites the online publication date and DOI without pages.
2. **Giacomelli DOI conflict:** the University of Padua repository reports DOI `10.4000/aes.3542`, while other current indexes report `10.4000/aes.3643`; the stable article URL is `/aes/3643`. Draft 16 omits the DOI rather than selecting a disputed value.
3. **Bamji year:** Consensus records 2018 because that is the online-first year; Wiley gives the print citation as 2019, vol. 33(5), 808–831. Draft 16's 2019 print citation is retained.

## Chronological-use audit

Consensus reinforces an important distinction already present in the paper: Iordanou, Pugliano, Taape, Bamji and Giacomelli are later-period scholarship or later-period evidence. They may establish persistence, availability or an alternative mechanism, but none is allowed to repair a missing 1404–1438 matrix cell. Greshko is a modern experiment and likewise carries no provenance weight. Davis is the principal modern technical anchor among this group for manuscript-internal palaeographic/codicological claims.

## Non-Consensus class

Manuscript descriptions and provenance claims for PAL, BSB, KdiH, Manuscripta.at, Wellcome, the Biblioteca Casanatense, the University of Padua, and archival/institutional records were not treated as Consensus failures. They require direct catalogue or primary-source verification. The Draft 16 hybrid and Vienna-herbal additions are grounded in those institutional/specialist records and are kept distinct from the Consensus-reviewed journal literature.

## Verdict

**No load-bearing scholarly citation reviewed by Consensus was falsified.** The audit forced two bibliographic corrections (Greshko pagination; Giacomelli DOI omission) and reinforced chronological downgrades already required by the Fortune protocol.
