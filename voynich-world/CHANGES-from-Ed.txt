VOYNICH WORLD v2 - placeholders replaced with Voynich cut-outs
===============================================================

Your v2, unchanged except for what is listed here. It still runs by double-clicking index.html.

WHAT IS IN IT  (the smaller download, voynich-world-v2-cutouts.zip, leaves out the PNG originals;
               it runs the same, but you need the -with-pngs zip to change pictures)
  plants/   120 plant cut-outs, one per herbal folio, named f12r_plant.png etc.
  nymphs/   17 bathing figures from f75r-f77r (some files are groups of 2-3 figures)
  animals/  the f76v creature, the Pisces fish, three bathers-in-barrels from f70v2 (not used yet)
  Cut from Yale's full-resolution scans of Beinecke MS 408 with hand-checked (SAM-assisted) masks,
  so the edges are clean and there are no holes. Stray specks were removed; a thin parchment
  fringe remains on some edges.

WHY THE PICTURES ARE ALSO IN images/*.js
  Chrome blocks loose PNG files as textures on a page opened from disk (file://) - tested with
  your three.js r149: "Access to image ... from origin 'null' has been blocked".
  Pictures stored inside a .js file are allowed. tools/pack_images.py builds images/plants.js,
  images/nymphs.js and images/animals.js from the PNG folders (640 px WebP; the landmark f93r at
  1024 px). After you add or change a PNG:   python tools/pack_images.py --big f93r_plant=1024
  (needs Python + Pillow). If you serve the folder from a web server you could load the PNGs
  directly instead, but the packed files work either way.

CODE CHANGES (small, marked in the files)
  index.html       loads images/plants.js, images/nymphs.js, images/animals.js and js/images.js
  js/images.js     NEW: turns the packed pictures into the same [{id, aspect, texture}] list your
                   placeholders return; group names for the plant sets
  js/main.js       kinds = makeImageKinds() || makePlaceholderKinds()   (falls back if images/ is missing)
  js/plants.js     sets may use group names: 'tall', 'mid', 'wide', 'all'
  js/nymphs.js     uses the nymph cut-outs, each at its own width (was a fixed 0.5 x height)
  js/settings.js   plants.sets -> groups; landmarkKind -> 'f93r_plant';
                   nymphs.pictures -> which nymph files to use (single figures only for now)

PHONES AND TABLETS (added after the first version)
  js/touch.js      NEW: only switches on for touch screens. Left thumb = joystick (appears under your
                   thumb on the lower-left; push part-way to walk slowly), right thumb = drag to look,
                   buttons for Autopilot, Fly/Walk and up/down while flying. The panel starts folded.
  js/player.js     reads the joystick (player.stick, -1..1) alongside the keys; walking speed follows
                   how far it is pushed. Keyboard behaviour is unchanged.
  js/images.js     on touch screens pictures are shrunk to settings.touch.maxPicture (384 px) to save memory
  js/main.js       pixel ratio capped at 1.5 on touch screens (2 on desktop, as before)
  js/settings.js   new 'touch' block (lookSpeed, stickSize, maxPicture)

QUALITY AND SPEED (third round) - all switches in settings.js -> quality
  js/quality.js    NEW: shared pieces below, and the automatic resolution.
  Shading          plants are lit from the sun (the card facing the sun is brighter), darker near the roots,
                   and each plant has a slightly different tone. Plant pictures are now 768 px (were 640).
  Shadows          every plant casts its own silhouette onto the ground, away from the sun, lying on the slopes;
                   they follow the time of day (long in the evening, gone at night) and fade with distance.
                   Never shorter than shadowMinLength x height, so noon still has shadows.
  Ground           paper grain and fibres up close; hollows and the foot of the hills slightly darker;
                   the smooth ground is now one shared mesh (6x fewer vertices, same shape).
  Water            ponds take the sky colour at a glance and ripple.
  Speed            the mist's slow swirl is worked out per mesh point instead of per pixel (its cost roughly
                   halved); only the plants in view and inside the fog are drawn; and the resolution adapts
                   to hold settings.quality.targetFps (60). The HUD now shows fps (and 'res 80%' when lowered).
                   Recording mode (R) always keeps full resolution.
  Changed files    groundfog.js (swirl in the vertex shader), terrain.js (shared mesh, hollows, paper, water),
                   plants.js (lighting, shadows, culling), sky.js (passes the sun on), main.js (camera position,
                   resolution, fps in HUD), settings.js (quality block), index.html (loads js/quality.js).

PLANT PICKER (fourth round)
  picker.html      NEW: open it like index.html (double-click). All 120 plants with their folio and section.
                   Choose a place (Path, Edge, Grove, Pond, Meadow, Glade), click plants to put them there,
                   pick the landmark and which bathing figures to use. Filter by section or shape; the closer
                   look links to Yale's whole page. "Try it in the world" opens the world with your choice;
                   "Copy for settings.js" gives the lines to paste so it sticks. Remembered in your browser.
  images/picture-info.js  NEW: folio, section and Yale id per picture (for the picker)
  js/main.js       reads the picker's choice from the address (index.html#pick=...)
  js/plants.js     a place whose list is empty [] now stays bare (settings: plants.emptyZonesBare)
