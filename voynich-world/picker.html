Plant picker

Choose a place, then click plants to put them there (click again to take them out). A plant can grow in several places. A place with nothing chosen stays bare. Try it opens the world with your choice; Copy for settings.js gives the text to keep it. Keys: 1-6 places, 7 landmark, 8 nymphs. Your choice is remembered in this browser.
