# JLCD v0.1 protocol amendment A — specificity sample-size bound

Date: 2026-08-29
Status: frozen before first execution.

The phrase “not explained solely by having a materially larger eligible sample” in T3 is quantified as follows before any JLCD output is generated:

- rank strip-pair controls by bias-corrected CMI effect (`observed - null mean`), not z;
- `{e,i}` must lie in the top quartile of that effect ranking;
- its eligible occurrence count must be no more than 2.0 times the median eligible occurrence count of non-e/i strip-pair controls.

This amendment changes no primary T1 or T2 decision rule.
