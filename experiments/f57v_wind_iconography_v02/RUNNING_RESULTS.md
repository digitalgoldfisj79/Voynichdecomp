The strongest alternative is **not another single iconographic label**. Medieval scientific diagrams repeatedly correlate winds, directions, seasons, elements, humours, qualities, ages and astronomical/calendar cycles in a single wheel. The best-supported model class is now:

> **Integrated tetradic concordance / cosmological-computational rota:** the concentric rings align several cyclical or fourfold systems, while the four humans may be personifications, operators, pointers, counters or state markers rather than four equivalent nouns.

### Decisive evidence for the model shift

1. **Henry Daniel, `Liber uricrisiarum`** — Cambridge MS Ff.2.6 f17r is explicitly catalogued as a diagram of quaternities correlating **humours, regions, winds, seasons, elements and stages of life** through hot/cold and dry/moist qualities. Henry's work was composed/revised c.1375–1382.

2. **Wellcome MS225, early 15th century** — the catalogue explicitly records f16v as a **circular pen-drawn figure of the Seasons, Winds, Humours, etc.** This places multi-domain quaternity wheels directly in the early fifteenth-century medical manuscript environment.

3. **NYPL MA069 f38v, 1240–1260** — catalogued as a circular chart correlating **directions, winds, elements, seasons, temperaments, qualities and ages**. This demonstrates that several apparently competing hypotheses can literally be layers of one diagram.

4. **BnF Latin 6556, Milan, mid-14th century** — a compact scientific sequence combines a wind rose, cosmography, cardinal points, zodiac, solar course, seasonal quarters and qualities, lunar/solar cycles, calendars and chronometry. F4v explicitly integrates the zodiac, cardinal points, the sun's path, equal day/night and seasonal qualitative information. Ff6v–7r use concentric circles for hours/planets/days. This is the strongest near-geographic evidence for multi-layer annular scientific diagramming.

5. **Royal MS 19 C I, `Breviari d'Amor`, early 14th century** — the same manuscript separately contains a universe with two angels turning its axes (f34v), four angels holding elemental/planetary spheres (f50r), and a wind diagram (f51r). Therefore anthropomorphs physically interacting with a cosmological diagram cannot automatically be identified as winds.

6. **Isidorian `Mundus–Annus–Homo` tradition** — circular diagrams use crossed opposition axes to correlate elements, seasons, humours, directions and primary qualities. This gives a historically grounded structural home for a 2+2 opposed-axis distinction, although no exact f57v facing pattern has been found.

7. **Morgan M.925, northern Italy / possibly Piacenza, 1018–1032** — finger-reckoning figures demonstrate that hands in scientific manuscripts can carry numerical information, while the same computus codex contains circular computational, wind and prognostic diagrams. This creates a specific, falsifiable hand-shape hypothesis.

8. **Four-position Fortune wheels, ca.1340–1350** — four humans around a circle can deliberately encode unequal cyclic states (ascending, reigning, descending, fallen). This is a structural control for the assumption that four axial humans must be equivalent personifications.
