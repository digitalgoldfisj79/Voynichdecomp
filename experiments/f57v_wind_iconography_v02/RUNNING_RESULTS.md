# RUNNING RESULTS — f57v Wind Iconography v0.2
Date: 2026-08-09

## Current endpoint status

### E1 — Historical existence: PASS
Multiple independently attested medieval wind witnesses instantiate the compound grammar of four principal winds in a circular/cosmological framework with materially active hands/arms and a subordinate-wind hierarchy.

Strongest examples:
- Venice, Biblioteca Marciana, MS lat. VIII.22 (2760), fol. **2r** (`Astronomy of Nemrod`, ca. 1200): Draelants identifies this folio as `Four cosmic winds`; Obrist describes the four cardinal winds standing on the airy stratum of concentric rings around the earth, with outstretched hands holding collateral-wind heads; the associated textual hierarchy is four principals plus eight collaterals = twelve. Earlier notes incorrectly gave the folio as 2v; this is corrected here to 2r.
- Cambridge, Gonville & Caius College Library, MS 428, fol. 39v (11th–12th c.): Raff describes four principal winds as full figures holding the eight collateral winds as winged heads in their hands.
- Heidelberg, UB, Cod. Sal. X,16, fol. 2v (`Scivias`, ca. 1195–1225): four winged principal figures participate in a circular annual/cosmological scheme and hold subordinate wind heads in their extended hands.

### E2 — Exact hierarchy: PASS historically, but no longer a target-level match
The exact 4 + 8 = 12 representational hierarchy, with four principal figures holding subordinate wind heads, is independently attested historically. However, the corrected f57v arm/gesture audit does **not** support four equivalent principals each with two collaterals. That earlier target-level analogy is retracted.

### E3 — Chronological bridge: SUBSTANTIALLY BRIDGED AT COMPONENT LEVEL; EXACT 4+8 HELD-HEAD ROTA STILL OPEN
The exact hand-held-head compound is securely attested in the 11th–early 13th centuries. Subsequent evidence supports transmission by iconographic transformation rather than abrupt disappearance:

- **Turin, Biblioteca Civica, Bosio B.176, fol. 3v, end 13th c.**: another `Liber Nemroth` witness, attributed in catalogue tradition to a Florentine or Sienese milieu; exact morphology pending.
- **Metropolitan Museum, Austria, ca.1300, `Four Winds Diagram`**: four heads in roundels personify principal winds in a cosmological/natural-science rota.
- **Isolated ca.1300 Petrus-Pictaviensis-related leaf**: Raff records four principal wind heads plus two subordinate wind personifications in each surrounding sector, retaining 4 + 8 while changing subordinate morphology.
- **Linz Cod.490 -> Vienna ÖNB Cod.364 f.4v, ca.1330**: a twelve-wind/four-principal world scheme is transformed; in Vienna the smaller figures are reinterpreted as four Paradise rivers while inherited wind names/horns persist.
- **British Library Yates Thompson MS 10 f.11v, ca.1370–1390, Paris**: official catalogue states that four angels hold winds symbolised by animal heads.
- **Giusto de' Menabuoi, Padua Baptistery, 1375–1378**: securely dated Padua Apocalypse cycle includes `Four winds`; dedicated pixel audit supports hand/control interaction with detached heads, treated as auxiliary rather than catalogue-level morphology.

Thus component morphology survives to within roughly one generation of the Voynich window in Padua itself, although the unchanged early 4+8 held-head rota has not been bridged into 1400–1450.

### E4 — Late-medieval component continuity: PASS
Wind personification and wind diagrams survive into and beyond the Voynich period. Relevant continuity includes Dati's `La sfera` (Florence, composed ca.1420–1430), Kyeser's `Bellifortis` (Besançon BM MS 1360 f.4v, Germany, 1400–1449), later Florentine `La sfera` witnesses, Padua Bodl.646 ca.1460, and other later-fifteenth-century wind-head diagrams.

### E5 — Geographic sensitivity: STRONGLY BRIDGED FOR COMPONENTS; OPEN FOR EXACT COMPOUND
Northern/central Italian and Alpine/Germanic continuity is secure at component level. The exact early compound remains open.

## Corrected f57v target morphology
The earlier `near-4x2` arm interpretation is **retracted**. N/E/S/W are page positions only, not semantic cardinal directions.

- positional N: faces **outward**; two secure gestures approximately page-E and page-W;
- positional E: faces **inward**; one secure gesture approximately page-W;
- positional S: faces **outward**; two secure gestures approximately page-E and page-W;
- positional W: faces **inward**; one secure gesture approximately page-S.

Secure ensemble: **2-1-2-1 gesture count** plus an opposed-axis facing distinction:
- vertical pair N/S = outward-facing + two gestures;
- horizontal pair E/W = inward-facing + one secure gesture.

The E/W gesture vectors are not rotationally symmetric. No secure held heads and no secure breath jets are observed. The centre remains coded only as a six-lobed/scalloped object with central dot.

## Deep research on alternatives — new result
Full synthesis: `DEEP_RESEARCH_ALTERNATIVES.md`.

The main result is a change in the *type of question*. The current evidence no longer favours assuming that the four f57v humans must be four equivalent personifications. A historically grounded alternative is that they are **functional agents / operators / markers** embedded in a cosmological or computational rota.

### Strongest matched control: Royal MS 19 C I, `Breviari d'Amor`
The British Library catalogue distinguishes within one early-14th-century scientific-encyclopaedic manuscript:
- f.34v: universe with **two angels turning the axes**;
- f.50r: spheres of the elements and planets with **four angels holding them**;
- f.51r: a **separate wind diagram**;
- f.53r: daylight hours;
- f.53v: circular planets/days-of-week diagram;
- f.54v: seasons.

This is a decisive control against the shortcut `four anthropomorphs physically interacting with a cosmological circle = winds`. In the same codex, agents physically operate/hold the cosmos and winds are represented separately.

### Ranked alternatives after deep research
1. **Cosmological operators / movers / supporters** — strongest functional fit.
2. **Paired-opposition natural philosophy (elements / qualities / seasons / humours)** — strongest semantic architecture; medieval diagrams explicitly distribute correlated quaternities across two crossed axes, and BnF Latin 6556 places this language in 14th-century Milan.
3. **Gestural computation / finger reckoning** — strongest hand-specific alternative. Morgan M.925 (Italy, possibly Piacenza) proves that male half-figures and isolated hands can encode numbers through finger positions and can combine numerical gestures with pointing/head turns inside a scientific/computistical manuscript culture.
4. **Cyclic-state / Wheel-of-Fortune-like state machine** — strongest proof that four humans at orthogonal positions may be intentionally non-equivalent; 14th-century Fortune wheels encode ascending, reigning, descending and fallen states around one wheel.
5. **Annus / weather / time-of-day hybrid cosmology** — strong multi-ring architecture; one circular diagram can overlay winds, weather, seasons, zodiac, light/dark and solar/lunar material.
6. **Cardinal directions / world quarters** — plausible, but historically entangled with winds and does not explain the gesture asymmetry alone.
7. **Astronomical / lunar / calendrical computation** — strong annular substrate, especially given Austrian `De sphaera` ca.1425 and Milanese 14th-century cosmography, but needs an operator model for the humans.
8. **Prognostic/divinatory wheel** — architectural possibility; e.g. the Sphere of Petosiris combines concentric circles with four cardinal circles.
9. **Four Rivers of Paradise** — broad graphic grammar remains relevant, but corrected 2-1-2-1 morphology fits worse than the explicit 2-2-2-2 Troyes comparator.
10. **Theological quaternity** — low without attributes.

A **transformed wind/cosmography hybrid remains live**. A *conventional* wind rota is no longer the default morphological interpretation.

### Most important period/geographic evidence
- **Milan, BnF Latin 6556, 14th c.**: cosmographic diagrams combine cardinal points, zodiac, solar course, seasons and qualitative natural-philosophical information; the manuscript includes a tract on the four elements.
- **Austria, Morgan M.722, ca.1425**: concentric planetary/zodiac diagrams lie inside the Voynich radiocarbon period.
These establish that complex annular cosmological/computational diagramming with correlated cycles was active in relevant central/northern-European and northern-Italian environments.

## Current scientific position
The historical-impossibility objection remains falsified: f57v can legitimately be compared to medieval wind/cosmological diagram traditions.

However, the corrected target morphology, Phase-3 adversarial search and alternative-population deep research **weaken the narrower claim that f57v is a straightforward conventional wind rota**.

The leading working model is now functional rather than icon-name driven:

> f57v may be a cosmological/computational rota in which the four humans are agents, pointers, counters or state markers, while the rings carry the primary information.

This model explains why the four figures need not form one rotationally equivalent class. The immediate programme should test this functional hypothesis before conducting further semantic icon hunting.
