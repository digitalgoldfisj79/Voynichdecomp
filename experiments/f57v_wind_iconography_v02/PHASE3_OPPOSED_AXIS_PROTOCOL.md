# PHASE 3 PREREGISTRATION — opposed-axis morphology search
Date: 2026-08-09

## Frozen target morphology
Page-position labels only; no semantic cardinal assignment is assumed.

- North figure: faces outward; two secure gestures approximately west and east.
- East figure: faces inward; one secure gesture approximately west.
- South figure: faces outward; two secure gestures approximately west and east.
- West figure: faces inward; one secure gesture approximately south.

Thus the target is coded as an opposed-axis pattern rather than fourfold rotational symmetry:

- vertical pair N/S: outward-facing, 2 gestures each;
- horizontal pair E/W: inward-facing, 1 secure gesture each;
- gesture-count pattern: 2-1-2-1 around the circle.

The earlier near-4x2 interpretation is superseded and must not be used as positive evidence.

## Primary question
Does a medieval diagrammatic/iconographic population contain an independently attested motif with four axial anthropomorphs in which one opposed pair is systematically oriented outward and the perpendicular pair inward, ideally with differentiated gesture counts or directional roles?

## Search classes
1. wind / anemological diagrams and wind personifications;
2. four rivers of Paradise;
3. four elements / qualities / humours / seasons;
4. cardinal-direction / world-part personifications;
5. macrocosm-microcosm and cosmological rotae;
6. Apocalypse four-winds imagery;
7. zodiac / computus / astronomical circular diagrams;
8. theological/allegorical radial diagrams.

## Frozen feature code
For every candidate with four axial anthropomorphs:
- date and region;
- circular/concentric/radial organization;
- positions at N/E/S/W axes;
- facing of each figure: inward / outward / tangential / indeterminate;
- gesture count per figure: 0/1/2+ / indeterminate;
- gesture direction in page coordinates;
- explicit semantic labels;
- breath/wind instrument/wind-head tokens;
- central object/person/emblem;
- whether opposed pairs differ systematically;
- whether pattern matches f57v exactly, partially, or not at all.

## Endpoints
P3-E1: Find at least one pre-1500 independently catalogued diagram with a systematic opposed-pair facing distinction among four axial anthropomorphs.

P3-E2: Find at least one 1300-1450 witness with that distinction.

P3-E3: Find at least one northern-Italian / Alpine / Germanic witness with that distinction.

P3-E4: Determine whether the distinction is wind-specific or occurs in non-wind controls.

P3-E5: Determine whether any candidate also shows a 2-1-2-1 (or functionally equivalent alternating) gesture-count pattern.

## Decision rule
- Exact match: four axial anthropomorphs + opposed-pair facing polarity matching f57v + alternating/differentiated gesture roles.
- Strong partial: four axial anthropomorphs + opposed-pair facing polarity, gesture differentiation unresolved or different.
- Weak partial: four axial anthropomorphs with systematic opposed-pair functional differentiation but facing not recoverable.
- Non-match: generic fourfold/radial arrangement without opposed-pair differentiation.

No interpretation of f57v as wind, rivers, elements, etc. is permitted from geometry alone. The phase is designed first to establish whether the opposed-axis morphology itself has a historical analogue and then to test its semantic specificity.
