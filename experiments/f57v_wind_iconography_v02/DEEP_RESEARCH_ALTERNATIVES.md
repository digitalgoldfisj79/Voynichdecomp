# DEEP RESEARCH — ALTERNATIVES TO A CONVENTIONAL WIND ROTA
Date: 2026-08-09
Branch: `experiment/f57v-wind-iconography-v0.2-20260809`
Status: research synthesis after Phase 3 opposed-axis result

## Frozen target morphology

Page-position labels only; no cardinal semantics assumed.

- N: faces outward; 2 secure gestures, approximately E + W.
- E: faces inward; 1 secure gesture, approximately W.
- S: faces outward; 2 secure gestures, approximately E + W.
- W: faces inward; 1 secure gesture, approximately S.
- Circular/concentric framework present.
- Four axial anthropomorphs present.
- Central six-lobed/scalloped object with central dot present; **not** identified semantically.
- No secure breath jets.
- No secure held wind heads.

The strongest anomaly is therefore the **opposed-axis functional polarity** N/S versus E/W plus the 2-1-2-1 gesture count. Phase 3 found no qualified pre-1500 exact or strong-partial analogue for that full signature.

## Main deep-research result

The most important alternative is not another four-personification category. It is that the four figures are **diagrammatic agents/operators/markers**, while the concentric rings carry the principal cosmological or computational content.

The strongest control is British Library Royal MS 19 C I, `Breviari d'Amor`, early 14th century, southern France (Toulouse?). The institutional catalogue distinguishes, within the same scientific-encyclopaedic image sequence:

- f.34v: diagram of the universe with **two angels turning the axes**;
- f.50r: diagram of the spheres of the elements and planets with **four angels holding them**;
- f.51r: a **separate diagram of the winds**;
- f.53r: daylight hours;
- f.53v: circular planets/days-of-week diagram;
- f.54v: seasons.

This is a high-value matched control because it proves that anthropomorphs acting physically on a circular/concentric cosmological diagram need not personify winds, even in a codex that depicts winds immediately afterwards.

## Ranked alternatives

### A1. Cosmological operators / sphere movers / supporters — HIGH

**Historical basis**
- Royal MS 19 C I f.34v: two angels turn the axes of the universe.
- Royal MS 19 C I f.50r: four angels hold the spheres of elements and planets.
- Yates Thompson MS 31 is a late-14th-century Catalan `Breviari d'Amor` witness with the same broad illustrated cosmological tradition, though its exact operator morphology has not been independently re-catalogued here.

**What it explains on f57v**
- active hands matter structurally rather than decoratively;
- differentiated gestures need not imply four equivalent personified substances;
- humans can sit at the interface between concentric cosmology and directional operations;
- a central object can be a hub/body acted upon rather than a semantic emblem.

**Mismatch / risk**
- the best explicit operator figures are angels, whereas f57v has no secure wings/halos;
- no exact N/S-outward versus E/W-inward 2-1-2-1 analogue has been found.

**Current status:** strongest *functional* alternative.

### A2. Paired-opposition natural philosophy: elements / qualities / seasons / humours — HIGH

**Historical basis**
- Walters W.73 f.7v–8r uses wheel diagrams to relate four elements, four humours, four seasons, four qualities and four cardinal directions through intersecting arcs.
- `De macrocosmo et microcosmo` in Poitiers MS 184 explicitly uses four concentric circular bands divided into four quarters by two crossing lines; cardinal points, elements, seasons and humours occupy the quarters with their qualities.
- BnF Latin 6556 f.4v, made in Milan in the 14th century, combines cardinal points, zodiac, solar course, equal day/night and seasonal qualitative labels such as hot/humid in a cosmographic diagram. The manuscript itself contains a tract on the four elements.

**What it explains on f57v**
- a deliberate 2+2 axis distinction is native to medieval natural-philosophical diagrams because the system is built from paired oppositions (hot/cold, moist/dry and associated correspondences);
- concentric rings can carry multiple correlated quaternities rather than one iconographic subject;
- Milanese 14th-century evidence places this diagrammatic language in a geographically and chronologically relevant environment.

**Mismatch / risk**
- surviving examples usually encode the opposition through lines/arcs/labels, not through four human faces with the exact f57v posture pattern;
- the 2-1-2-1 hand pattern remains unexplained unless the figures are operators for the opposition system.

**Current status:** strongest *semantic-system* alternative.

### A3. Gestural computation / finger reckoning / human figures as numeric signs — MEDIUM-HIGH

**Historical basis**
Morgan MS M.925, Italy possibly Piacenza, 1018–1032, is unusually relevant:
- f.38r–v contains columns of hands encoding numbers by finger positions;
- f.38v combines hands with male half-figures in medallions performing numerical gestures;
- f.39r has male half-figures whose hand positions encode large numbers; some figures turn their heads and point with the other hand;
- the same computus collection includes circular cosmological/prognostic diagrams, including the Sphere of Petosiris at f.63v with three concentric circles and four cardinal circles.

**What it explains on f57v**
- the hands may be *notation*, not literal pointing at directions;
- asymmetrical hand counts/shapes can be intentional informational differences;
- profile orientation may be secondary to making the relevant hand visible;
- numerical/computistical content naturally belongs in circular or annular diagrams.

**Mismatch / risk**
- strongest explicit exemplar is early (11th c.);
- f57v gestures currently look more like extended arm/pointing actions than canonical compact finger-number poses;
- no four-figure circular dactylonomic rota matching f57v has been found.

**Current status:** serious and directly testable; should be tested before treating every endpoint as a directional arrow.

### A4. Cyclic-state / Wheel of Fortune / ages or stages — MEDIUM

**Historical basis**
Morgan M.48 f.37r (Paris, 1340–50) and M.324 f.34v (Paris, ca.1350) show a Wheel of Fortune with four male figures around the wheel in deliberately non-equivalent positional states: ascending, reigning at the top, descending, and recumbent at the bottom. Medieval Fortune iconography therefore supplies a clear population in which four humans at orthogonal positions encode **different operations/states**, not four equivalent cardinal personifications.

**What it explains on f57v**
- posture and facing asymmetry are expected rather than anomalous;
- figures may encode transitions in a cycle;
- annular text could describe states, phases or transitions around the cycle.

**Mismatch / risk**
- f57v lacks the standard visible Fortune-wheel narrative: no secure Fortune figure, king, ascent/descent sequence or wheel rim with riders;
- N/S versus E/W class structure does not map obviously onto the standard four Fortune states.

**Current status:** strongest proof-of-principle that four axial humans can be intentionally functionally unequal; semantic fit remains moderate.

### A5. Annus / weather / time-of-day / seasonal cosmology — MEDIUM

**Historical basis**
Heidelberg Cod. Sal. X,16 f.2v (`Annus`, ca.1195–1225) is a multi-ring circular programme combining:
- central Annus holding Sol and Luna;
- light/dark figures;
- four wind heads linking to a weather ring;
- weather phenomena;
- four seasonal-weather personifications placed opposite one another;
- zodiac signs;
- four full wind figures in the corners holding subordinate wind heads.

**What it explains on f57v**
- a single circular page can overlay several semantic classes at different radii;
- four axial figures may mark states of time/weather rather than being the subject of every ring;
- opposite placements can encode seasonal/day-night contrasts.

**Mismatch / risk**
- much earlier than Voynich;
- known example has abundant explicit semantic iconography absent on f57v.

**Current status:** strong architectural analogue, weaker direct semantic identification.

### A6. Cardinal directions / world quarters, distinct from meteorological winds — MEDIUM

**Historical basis**
Medieval wind/direction traditions frequently overlap, but later `La sfera` material explicitly frames the four personified winds as four directional `plagae` (eastern, northern, southern, western regions/quarters). BnF Latin 6556 likewise integrates cardinal points into broader cosmography.

**What it explains on f57v**
- axial placement without requiring visible breath;
- different figure orientations could mark relations between world quarters and another system.

**Mismatch / risk**
- direction and wind categories are historically entangled, so this may not be a genuinely independent alternative;
- simple cardinal-direction personification does not explain 2-1-2-1 gestures.

### A7. Astronomical / lunar / calendrical computation — MEDIUM

**Historical basis**
- Morgan M.722 (`De sphaera`, Austria, ca.1425) uses concentric planetary circles and zodiacal surrounds squarely within the Voynich radiocarbon period.
- BnF Latin 6556, Milan 14th c., combines solar calendar, zodiac, cardinal points, seasons and cosmography across adjacent diagrams.
- Walters W.73 contains winds, tides, lunar/planetary cycles and zodiac within a single compact scientific textbook.

**What it explains on f57v**
- concentric annular writing is highly compatible with cyclical astronomical/calendrical computation;
- central marker plus four agents may control or index a temporal system.

**Mismatch / risk**
- no secure zodiacal, lunar or planetary iconography on f57v;
- four differentiated humans remain unexplained without combining this with A1 or A3.

### A8. Prognostic/divinatory wheel — MEDIUM-LOW

Morgan M.925 f.63v, the `Sphere of Petosiris` / `De divinatione mortis et vitae`, consists of three concentric circles, four smaller circles at the four cardinal points and a central foliate cross. This proves that an annular/cardinal structure can be prognostic rather than meteorological. It lacks four anthropomorphic axial agents, so it is an architectural rather than morphological match.

### A9. Four Rivers of Paradise / theological-cosmological quaternity — LOW-MEDIUM after target correction

Troyes MS 252 f.14 remains a major hard negative because its four axial male river personifications each hold two subordinate medallions around a central theological structure. However, its secure pattern is 2-2-2-2 rather than f57v's 2-1-2-1, so the corrected target morphology lowers this alternative substantially. There are also no secure water vessels/streams on f57v.

### A10. Evangelists / Fathers / virtues / theological quaternity — LOW

Circular fourfold sacred arrangements are common enough to prevent exclusion on geometry alone, including four Evangelist-symbol/Father structures. But f57v has no secure Christian attributes, cross, halos, books or Evangelist animals, and the hand asymmetry is not naturally explained.

## Comparative conclusion

The deep research shifts the centre of gravity away from asking **which four things the four humans personify**.

A more productive model is:

> f57v may be a cosmological/computational rota in which the four humans are functional agents, pointers, counters or state markers, and the rings carry the main information.

This model naturally accommodates the otherwise troublesome fact that the four humans are **not one rotationally equivalent class**.

The strongest historical reason for making this shift is Royal MS 19 C I: the same early-14th-century scientific/encyclopaedic manuscript separately depicts (a) angels turning the universe, (b) four angels holding planetary/elemental spheres, and (c) a wind diagram. Four figures physically related to a cosmological circle therefore cannot be presumed to be winds.

## Current qualitative ranking

1. **Cosmological operators / movers / supporters** — strongest functional fit.
2. **Paired-opposition natural philosophy (elements/qualities/seasons/humours)** — strongest semantic architecture.
3. **Gestural computation / finger reckoning** — strongest hand-specific alternative and highly falsifiable.
4. **Cyclic-state / Fortune-like state machine** — strongest explanation for deliberately unequal postures.
5. **Annus/weather/time hybrid** — strong multi-ring architectural analogue.
6. **Cardinal directions/world quarters** — plausible but overlaps heavily with wind tradition.
7. **Astronomical/lunar/calendrical computation** — plausible annular substrate, weak figure explanation by itself.
8. **Prognostic/divinatory wheel** — architectural possibility.
9. **Four Rivers of Paradise** — broad grammar survives, exact corrected morphology is poorer.
10. **Theological quaternity** — low without attributes.

A **transformed wind/cosmography hybrid** remains live and should be compared with the alternatives above; a *conventional* wind rota is no longer the default morphology.

## Next decisive tests

### T1 — Hand-shape / dactylonomy test
Do not code hands as generic `pointing` initially. For each secure hand, record visible fingers, flexion/extension, palm orientation and contact with thumb. Compare blindly against medieval finger-reckoning sign tables (including Morgan M.925). If several f57v hands form recognized numerical signs, the interpretation changes radically.

### T2 — Endpoint-to-ring registration test
Map every secure fingertip/hand vector in image coordinates and extend the vector to the nearest annular boundary/text baseline. Test against rotated/randomized vectors. Question: do gestures land on reproducible word, glyph, divider or ring-boundary anchors more often than chance?

### T3 — Opposed-sector text test
Partition annular text by the frozen N/S versus E/W classes. Without semantic assumptions, compare token/glyph distributions, repeated sequences, entropy and transition profiles:
- N versus S similarity;
- E versus W similarity;
- within-class versus across-class distance.
A true paired-opposition diagram predicts measurable class structure in the surrounding text more naturally than a decorative four-personification model.

### T4 — Cyclic-state ordering test
Test whether local text adjacent to N→E→S→W behaves as an ordered progression rather than four interchangeable sectors. Compare cyclic transition statistics against all rotations/reflections.

### T5 — Operator-versus-personification iconographic panel
Build a matched visual panel of:
- cosmological sphere holders/movers;
- wind personifications;
- four-element/humour/season diagrams;
- Fortune/cyclic-state figures;
- reckoning figures.
Code hands, facing, relation to rings and equivalence/non-equivalence of figures, not general visual similarity.

## Falsification criteria

- A1 operator model weakens if hand vectors do not interact with rings/central structure and figures behave textually as four equivalent sectors.
- A2 paired-opposition model weakens if N/S and E/W text sectors show no greater within-pair similarity than permutation controls.
- A3 reckoning model is rejected if hand shapes fail recognized dactylonomic signs under blind coding.
- A4 cyclic-state model weakens if no ordered N→E→S→W or reverse sequence is detectable.
- Conventional wind model regains relative strength only if independent period-near wind witnesses reproduce the opposed-axis posture or if text/graphic evidence identifies wind-specific residues.
