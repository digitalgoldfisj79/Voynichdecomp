## Frozen Voynich target

After preregistration, ZLZI standard paragraph (`P0`) text was measured under the same within-line exact-token-multiset-preserving null:

- 3,885 lines; 33,200 tokens; 29,315 eligible adjacent pairs.
- non-exact character Levenshtein distance 1 (`N1_ED1`): observed 1,074; null 923.175 ± 25.165; **ratio 1.16338, z +5.994**.
- exact adjacency (`E1`): observed 281; null 275.981 ± 14.334; **ratio 1.01819, z +0.350**.

The preregistered generator N1 equivalence band was therefore [1.11338, 1.21338].

## G0 — exact published default, seed 19

Published generator output: 1,200 lines; 10,832 tokens; 9,632 adjacent pairs.

- N1_ED1: observed 474; null 384.530 ± 16.381; **ratio 1.23267, z +5.462**.
- E1 exact: observed 98; null 128.501 ± 9.773; **ratio 0.76264, z -3.121**.

Frozen verdict: **FAIL**.

Reason: the generator clearly creates a strong adjacent one-edit-neighbour ordering effect, but its N1 ratio is 0.0193 above the upper equivalence boundary and, more decisively, it produces significant exact-adjacency *avoidance* whereas the Voynich P0 corpus is at chance.

Descriptively, the default seed-19 absolute ED1 adjacency rate is 4.921% versus 3.664% in P0 VMS; its within-line null density is also higher (3.992% versus 3.149%).

## G1 — untouched published configuration, seeds 1–20

## Frozen mechanistic ablations

Ten paired seeds each.

### A1 — sourceChooser `page` -> `random`

- median N1 ratio **1.94156**; range 1.73977–2.04738.
- median default-minus-random = **-0.73987**; default higher 0/10.
- preregistered predicted-drop criterion: **FAIL**.

Absolute rates explain part of this apparently paradoxical result: random-source outputs have median observed N1 rate only 2.812%, but a much lower within-line null density of 1.463%. Random source selection therefore increases the *conditional ordering ratio* by making the line inventory less saturated with near-neighbour pairs; it does not reproduce the VMS absolute N1 rate.

Conclusion: page-local donor selection is not necessary for the tight-null adjacent N1 ratio.

### A2 — `method.morph.reuse_last.probability` 10 -> 0

- median N1 ratio **1.06099**; range 0.97319–1.14573.
- median paired default-minus-ablation = **+0.16409**.
- default higher in **10/10** seeds.
- preregistered predicted-drop criterion: **PASS**.

Median observed N1 rate is 4.340%, null 4.175%.

This is the decisive causal ablation. The published `reuse_last` branch is the principal source of the generator's tight-null adjacent near-neighbour excess.

In the pinned source, `reuseLastMorphedGroup` may immediately append a second word after a newly generated word. If the first contains a combinable ligature, it appends a split-derived group; otherwise it attempts to delete the prefix; in either case there is a 50% internal probability of another token replacement. This is an explicitly adjacent derivative/copy-modify operation.

### A3 — shipped `position` source chooser

- median N1 ratio **1.49506**; range 1.33341–1.56201.
- median default-minus-position = -0.27836; default higher 0/10.

Descriptive only by preregistration. More explicitly positional donor selection strengthens the normalized adjacent ED1 excess but still is not the primary causal channel identified by A2.

## Scientific conclusion

1. **The Timm near-neighbour phenomenon is independently confirmed.** VMS P0 has a robust tight-null excess of adjacent non-identical edit-distance-one tokens (1.163×, z+5.99).
2. **The actual published Timm–Schinner generator robustly generates the same qualitative phenomenon.** All 20 untouched seeds show significant tight-null N1 enrichment.
3. **The published implementation does not quantitatively reproduce the full Voynich adjacent-order signature.** It slightly overproduces the normalized N1 effect and, much more importantly, generates significant exact-repeat avoidance (~0.76×) where VMS P0 is exactly at chance (~1.02×).
4. **The component that causes our N1 statistic is specifically the explicit `reuse_last` adjacent derivative channel.** Removing that one 10% mechanism collapses the ratio to ~1.06; changing page-local donor selection to random does not abolish the ratio.
5. Therefore the correct claim is **mechanistic partial sufficiency, not identification**: an explicitly copy/modify-adjacent operation is sufficient to create the surviving Voynich-like near-neighbour anomaly, but Torsten Timm's published full generator is not an adequate model of the joint adjacent-order data under the stricter conditioning used here.

No conclusion about meaninglessness, plaintext, ciphering or historical scribal intent follows from this audit alone.
