## General correction: plausibility is not evidence

The programme must not score broad historical plausibility, manuscript content ecology, presence of German/transalpine people in northern Italy, or ordinary movement into Venice/Padua as evidence for the corridor.

The following receive **zero positive evidential weight**:

- coexistence of astronomy, astrology, medicine, optics, natural philosophy, diagrams, or other scientific subjects in a codex;
- the mere existence of a scribe styled `de Almania`;
- the mere existence of a book worker moving to Venice or Padua;
- a named Paduan scribe/patron/teacher network unless a cross-node edge is established;
- holding location used as a proxy for copying location.

A documentary/prosopographic item scores only if it establishes an actual edge relevant to the corridor, e.g. the same person/hand/exemplar/patron/workshop/teacher-pupil relation demonstrably linking two nodes. **Nodes without an edge score zero.**
