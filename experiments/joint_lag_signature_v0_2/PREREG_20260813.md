# Joint lag-signature programme v0.2 — preregistration

Date frozen: 2026-08-13, after v0.1 and before any v0.2 statistic is computed.
Branch: `experiment/joint-lag-signature-v0.2-20260813`.

## Why v0.2 exists

v0.1 established, under a within-line multiset-preserving null, that the same literal character-distance statistic replicates across transcription frames (N1), exact lag-2 repetition replicates, literal A–near-B–A BRIDGE triples do not carry their joint signal, and the d2 surplus is strongly interior to lines rather than at line starts. v0.1 also had two deliberately unresolved points: CHAIN5 failed calibration, and the top-K masking criterion confounded power loss with effect loss.

No v0.1 threshold is retroactively changed. v0.2 tests consequences opened by those results.

## Data, frames and null

Same `voynich_transcriptions_slim.json`, same 12 unique transcription contents frozen in v0.1. Reference frame ZLZI. Primary null remains **within-line exact-token-multiset-preserving permutation**. No cross-line pairs.

Primary ZLZI tests: 2,000 permutations. Cross-frame replication: 500 permutations. Seed 20260813 with disjoint deterministic offsets from v0.1.

### Running-text robustness subset

IVTFF locus main type `P` is running paragraph text; `L`, `C`, `R` are labels, circular and radial writing respectively. The supplied slim JSON stores the locus unit marker as `u`. A locus is P-running-text for this programme iff the two-character locus code following its leading marker has main type P, implemented as `len(u)>=2 and u[1]=='P'`.

P-only is a **targeted robustness test after seeing the all-locus v0.1 result**, not an independent discovery sample.

## Observables

All string comparisons use literal frame strings and unit-cost Levenshtein distance 1, as in v0.1.

- `E2`: x[i]==x[i+2].
- `E4`, `E6`: exact equality at lags 4 and 6.
- `N1`: x[i]!=x[i+1] and ED(x[i],x[i+1])=1.
- `E2_NONBRIDGE`: E2 event with ED(x[i],x[i+1]) != 1.
- `N1_NONCLOSURE`: N1 adjacent pair excluded if it touches either side of any observed lag-2 exact closure: exclude pair (i,i+1) when x[i]==x[i+2] or, when i>0, x[i-1]==x[i+1]. The identical operation is recomputed inside every permutation.
- position-specific E2 for start index i=0,1,2,3,4,5 and pooled i>=1.
- interior parity contrast: E2 opportunities with start i>=1 split by i mod 2, so the known i=0 boundary cell cannot generate an apparent parity result.
- `TOPK_CONTRAST(K)`: fixed global top-K token set from the observed corpus frequency ranking. Each exact lag-2 event is assigned to TOP if its repeated endpoint token is in the set, REST otherwise. Let mu_TOP and mu_REST be permutation means. Statistic D = log((count_TOP+0.5)/(mu_TOP+0.5)) - log((count_REST+0.5)/(mu_REST+0.5)); compare observed D to the same D over permutations. Also report each cell's enrichment ratio and the fraction of positive excess contributed by TOP.

## Controls — before downstream inference

### C6 boundary-gate positive

Start from a within-line shuffled ZLZI pseudo-corpus. At 2% of non-overlapping eligible **interior** d2 opportunities (i>=1), set x[i+2]=x[i], never inject at i=0. Analyse against fresh tight null. Required: pooled interior E2 ratio>=1.15,z>=3; boundary contrast z<=-3; start-cell enrichment lower than interior by >=1.25-fold. Failure invalidates H6.

### C7 separability controls

C7a coupled-only: shuffled pseudo-corpus; inject A–B–A at 2% non-overlapping sites with B a guaranteed ED1 neighbour of A. Required: raw E2 and N1 both z>=3; after the exclusion operations, E2_NONBRIDGE and N1_NONCLOSURE must each have ratio<=1.10 or |z|<2. This proves the exclusions remove a genuinely coupled mechanism.

C7b independent: shuffled pseudo-corpus; inject lag-2 exact closures at one set of sites with the bridge forced non-ED1, and inject adjacent ED1 pairs at a disjoint set of sites not touching any d2 closure. Required: E2_NONBRIDGE ratio>=1.15,z>=3 and N1_NONCLOSURE ratio>=1.10,z>=3. Failure invalidates H7.

### C8 parity persistence positive

Shuffled pseudo-corpus; at 2% non-overlapping seven-token sites impose x[i]=x[i+2]=x[i+4]=x[i+6]. Required: E4 and E6 each ratio>=1.15,z>=3. Failure invalidates H8.

### C9 lexical contrast null/positive

C9a null calibration: 20 shuffled pseudo-corpora; for K=20 TOPK_CONTRAST must have mean z near zero (absolute mean <=0.5) and no more than 2/20 |z|>=2.
C9b positive: shuffled pseudo-corpus; inject E2 only when the anchor token is in the fixed top-20 set. Required K20 contrast z>=3. Failure invalidates H9.

## Hypotheses

### H6 — line-start gate, not generic parity

The d2 residual is suppressed specifically at the start of lines and elevated after the first word; the v0.1 odd/even appearance is secondary to including i=0 in the even cell.

SUPPORT iff on ZLZI all of:
1. i=0 E2 enrichment <=0.80;
2. pooled i>=1 E2 enrichment >=1.20 and z>=3;
3. boundary contrast |z|>=3 and interior/start enrichment fold>=1.50;
4. **interior-only parity contrast |z|<2 or fold<1.15**.

Robustness condition: the same qualitative boundary direction (start enrichment < interior enrichment) must hold in >=10/12 unique frames, and the P-only ZLZI subset must have interior ratio>=1.15,z>=2 and start<interior. If the primary four conditions pass but P-only fails, verdict is `ALL-LOCUS ONLY`, not support.

FALSIFY iff ZLZI start and interior enrichment differ by <1.15-fold with |contrast z|<2, or P-only reverses direction with z>=2.

### H7 — separable local ordering effects

The lag-1 N1 surplus and lag-2 exact surplus are not mainly the same local A–near-B–A events; they remain after mutually removing closure-overlap.

SUPPORT iff ZLZI E2_NONBRIDGE ratio>=1.15,z>=3 AND N1_NONCLOSURE ratio>=1.10,z>=3, with both ratios>1 in >=10/12 unique frames and z>=2 in >=8/12.

FALSIFY iff either residual falls to ratio<=1.05 with |z|<2 in ZLZI and does not replicate direction in >=8/12 frames. Otherwise unresolved.

### H8 — persistent interleaved parity memory

If lag-2 exact attraction reflects a persistent odd/even state, it should leave broad exact attraction at the next parity-compatible distances.

SUPPORT iff E4>=1.15,z>=2 AND E6>=1.15,z>=2 on ZLZI.
FALSIFY iff E4<=1.10 and E6<=1.10 and neither has z>=2. Otherwise unresolved.

This replaces the v0.1 CHAIN5 test that failed calibration.

### H9 — high-frequency lexical carrier, corrected

Top-frequency token types are **disproportionately enriched** among lag-2 exact events, beyond merely providing many opportunities.

Primary K=20. SUPPORT iff TOPK_CONTRAST z>=3 and top-20 accounts for >=60% of the positive E2 excess above its permutation expectation. FALSIFY iff |contrast z|<2 while REST itself retains E2 ratio>=1.10,z>=2. Otherwise unresolved. K=5 and 50 are sensitivity analyses, not additional confirmatory tests.

## Decision order

Controls first. H6 next because it conditions the mechanistic geometry. H7 then decides one-versus-two local effects. H8 tests persistence beyond lag 2. H9 tests lexical concentration without the v0.1 power confound.

No explanatory generator will be fitted in v0.2. A generator is warranted only if a specific geometry survives H6–H9.
