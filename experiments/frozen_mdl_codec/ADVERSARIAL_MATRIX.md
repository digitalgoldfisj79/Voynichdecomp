It does not test mixed unit sizes, unequal class sizes, nulls, multiple keys, registered house/gallows constraints, or the frozen adjacent-length selector. Those require the next class-specific calibration layer.

The current evidence register remains `INTERNAL` until a separate model has reproduced and demolished the complete frozen package.
