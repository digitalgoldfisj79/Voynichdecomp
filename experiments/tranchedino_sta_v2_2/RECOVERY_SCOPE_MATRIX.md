## Adjudication

The direct full-STA alphabetic arm is closed by H21. The old same-family ordering carrier remains closed and is not revived by STA labels. M19 remains closed. Generic mixed-unit/nomenclator and segmented-polygraphic solvers failed their own calibration and therefore provide no admissible Voynich negative.

The only material historical/representation gap left in the Tranchedino line is a **prospectively qualified, empirically constrained mixed-unit system** in which alphabetic homophones, null/code events and any variable-length unitisation are derived from Cod. 2398 rather than arbitrary synthetic priors. Such a Stage B must first solve fresh controls at the intended observation rate and must stop before Voynich if codeword identity or segmentation is not recoverable.
