# Joint lag-signature programme v0.1 — preregistration

Date frozen: 2026-08-13, before execution on the VMS statistics below.
Branch: `experiment/joint-lag-signature-v0.1-20260813`

## Prior-art gate

This programme does **not** retest the already-run July arms A–G/G' as if they were new. Prior work established that ordinary sequential/local copy-mutate and independent positional-template models fail the old lag shape, while a regional-reuse + partial-double-substitution + rare-near-forming model (G') reproduced the **old page/loose-null** profile. Session 2 on 2026-08-13 changed the estimand: the live residual is defined against a **within-line multiset-preserving permutation null**. Under that tight null, lag-1 exact is chance, lag-2 exact is elevated, and the class-level EVA edge decomposition is withdrawn. The programme therefore tests ordering constraints that survive conditioning on each line's exact token multiset.

External prior art to record, not claim as novel: Timm/Schinner self-citation and similar-word co-occurrence work; Grove/line-position effects; symbol autocorrelation work. Novelty claims are out of scope for this run.

## Data and tokenization

Primary source: `voynich_transcriptions_slim.json` (VTC slim). A line is the whitespace-tokenized `t[FRAME]` string exactly as supplied; no EVA-specific segmentation or glyph-class recoding is used. Empty tokens are dropped. No cross-line pairs are formed.

Reference frame: `ZLZI`.
Cross-frame set: every frame with >=10,000 tokens and >=1,000 eligible lag-2 pairs. The eligibility rule is frozen before statistics are computed.

## Null

Unless a test explicitly states otherwise, **tight null = within-line multiset-preserving permutation**: independently permute the tokens already present in each line, preserving line length and its exact token multiset. This asks only whether the observed ordering is exceptional given the line's contents.

Monte Carlo: 500 permutations for cross-frame tests; 2,000 for final ZLZI reference estimates. Seed 20260813. Report actual count/rate, null mean, null SD, ratio actual/null, and z. A zero null SD is reported as unresolved rather than infinite evidence.

## Frame-free observables

All are defined identically in every transcription frame.

- `E1`: exact equality at lag 1: x[i] == x[i+1].
- `E2`: exact equality at lag 2: x[i] == x[i+2].
- `E4`: exact equality at lag 4.
- `N1`: non-exact Levenshtein distance exactly 1 at lag 1.
- `BRIDGE`: x[i] == x[i+2], x[i] != x[i+1], and Levenshtein(x[i],x[i+1]) == 1. This is an A–B–A triple whose bridge B is a one-edit neighbour of A.
- `CHAIN5`: x[i] == x[i+2] == x[i+4]. This detects extension of the same parity chain (A–?–A–?–A).
- `ISO_ABA`: x[i] == x[i+2], x[i] != x[i+1], but the same A does not continue at i+4 when that position exists. Boundary cases with no i+4 are excluded from the confirmatory statistic.

Levenshtein is ordinary character-level unit-cost insertion/deletion/substitution on the literal frame string. This is deliberately not an EVA edge/interior taxonomy.

## Known-answer controls — run before VMS inference

C0 null calibration: create 20 pseudo-corpora by independently shuffling every observed ZLZI line once. Analyse each pseudo-corpus against fresh within-line shuffles. For E2/N1/BRIDGE/CHAIN5, mean ratio must lie in [0.95,1.05] and no more than 2/20 replicates may have |z|>=2. Failure invalidates that statistic.

C1 ABA-only positive: from a shuffled ZLZI pseudo-corpus, at 2% of non-overlapping eligible locations set x[i+2]=x[i] while leaving the middle token otherwise untouched. E2 must reach z>=3 and ratio>=1.15. BRIDGE is **not** required to rise.

C2 bridge positive: at 2% of non-overlapping eligible locations set x[i+2]=x[i] and replace x[i+1] by a deterministic one-character substitution of x[i] (append `~` if needed to guarantee distance 1). E2 and BRIDGE must each reach z>=3 and ratio>=1.15.

C3 parity-chain positive: at 2% of non-overlapping eligible locations set x[i+2]=x[i] and x[i+4]=x[i]. E2, E4 and CHAIN5 must each reach z>=3 and ratio>=1.15.

A hypothesis test whose own relevant control fails carries no information.

## Hypotheses and falsifiers

### H0 — single-statistic frame robustness

The coarse lag-1 near-neighbour finding is a genuine ordering property rather than a class-taxonomy artefact.

Prediction: the **same N1 statistic** is enriched across transcription frames; E2 independently replicates.

H0-N1 passes iff, among eligible frames, >=80% have N1 ratio>1.00, >=2/3 have ratio>=1.10 and z>=2, and the median ratio>=1.10. Otherwise the near-neighbour half of the joint target is withdrawn as frame-free.

H0-E2 passes iff >=80% of eligible frames have E2 ratio>=1.10 and z>=2. This is a revalidation, not a novelty claim.

### H1 — coupled bridge / one-mechanism hypothesis

The lag-1 near-similarity and lag-2 exact effects are coupled: the common local object is preferentially A–B–A with B a one-edit neighbour of A.

Support criterion: on ZLZI BRIDGE ratio>=1.20 and z>=3, and across eligible frames >=2/3 show ratio>1 with median ratio>=1.10. Strong support: >=2/3 also have z>=2.

Falsification: ZLZI BRIDGE ratio<=1.10 or |z|<2 **and** cross-frame median<=1.05. A failed H1 means the two surviving aggregate effects should be modelled as separable until contrary evidence appears.

### H2 — two interleaved persistent chains

Odd and even positions behave like two interleaved persistent states/chains. A genuine persistent parity chain should not create only one isolated d2 echo; it should propagate to d4 and to A–?–A–?–A chains.

Support: ZLZI E4 ratio>=1.15,z>=2 AND CHAIN5 ratio>=1.25,z>=2.
Falsify: E4 ratio<=1.10 AND CHAIN5 ratio<=1.10 or z<2 for both. Mixed outcome = unresolved.

### H3 — isolated order-2 motif closure

The d2 effect is predominantly a local three-token closure (A–B–A) rather than a persistent parity chain.

Support: H0-E2 passes, H2 is falsified, and ISO_ABA ratio>=1.15,z>=3 on ZLZI. Cross-frame direction is secondary. This supports an order-2 closure family only; it does not identify semantics or scribal intent.

### H4 — fixed line-phase / boundary mechanism

The d2 excess is tied to a fixed phase or line boundary rather than being spread through line interiors.

Two pre-specified contrasts on ZLZI:
1. start-index parity: observed difference between even-start and odd-start E2 counts, standardized against the same contrast under tight permutations;
2. line-start versus interior: excess at i=0 versus i>=1, standardized against permutations.

Support H4 iff either contrast has |z|>=3.0 (Bonferroni-safe for two tests at roughly familywise 0.005) AND the corresponding E2 ratio contrast is >=1.25-fold. Otherwise H4 is unsupported. Report both regardless.

### H5 — small lexical-carrier hypothesis

A few high-frequency repeated token types carry the E2 residual.

Rank token types by global ZLZI frequency before pair selection. Recompute E2 after masking candidate lag-2 pairs whose repeated endpoint token is among top K, K={5,20,50}; positions are not collapsed.

Support lexical-carrier H5 iff K=20 reduces E2 to ratio<1.05 or |z|<2. Falsify H5 iff even K=50 retains ratio>=1.15 and z>=2. Otherwise unresolved.

## Decision logic

1. Controls first. Any failed control invalidates its downstream statistic.
2. H0 frame gate second. If N1 fails, do not claim a frame-free joint mechanism; continue E2-only tests H2–H5.
3. H1 decides whether the N1 and E2 effects can be unified locally.
4. H2 vs H3 distinguishes persistent parity memory from isolated order-2 closure.
5. H4 asks whether the closure is a fixed line-phase/boundary effect.
6. H5 asks whether a small token vocabulary explains the residual.

No generator is fitted to the VMS in v0.1. If a mechanism survives, a v0.2 generator will be built from the surviving constraints with parameters estimated on training pages and evaluated on held-out pages.
