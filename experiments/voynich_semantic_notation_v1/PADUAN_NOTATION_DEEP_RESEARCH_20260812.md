# VSN-v1 Workstream B — Deep Research on Northern-Italian Compositional Technical Notation

Date: 2026-08-12
Status: substantive deep-research closeout; direct manuscript-image inspection remains incomplete for several witnesses.
Scope: northern Italy, especially Padua/Veneto/Mantua/Vicenza, c. 1350–1450. No Voynich-adjacent literature was used to generate the historical candidates.

## 1. Executive result

The first-pass historical conclusion was too weak and partly rediscovered material already present in the wider programme. A deeper search changes Workstream B materially.

The strongest evidence is not a generic analogy to medieval abbreviation or mensural music. It is a compact early-fifteenth-century northern-Italian cluster of explicitly **compositional information-encoding techniques**:

1. **Padua, 1420/1423 — Matteo da Verona, _De arte memorandi_**: artificial words are made by concatenating initial syllables from selected key words; commercial quantities and dates are assigned to fixed body-part fields; and physicians’ primary qualities are encoded by category, side and ordinal position on four-column/four-degree structures.
2. **Venice, 1424 — Vat. lat. 10488**: an ordinary abbacus/mercantile mathematical manuscript uses algebraic abbreviations, coefficients, formal fractions, spatial/superscript devices and occasional schematic equation-like layouts in practical computation.
3. **Mantua, 1429 — Bartolomeo da Mantova, _Liber memoriae artificialis_**: four ordered source items are reduced to an artificial four-syllable codeword by concatenating their initial syllables. The manuscripts also contain formal symbol/attribute systems for numbers, dates, debts and credits.
4. **Vicenza/Mantua, 1434 — Jacopo Ragona, _Artificialis memoriae regulae_**: a practical record is decomposed into ordered fields — day, month, year, debtor, father, lineage, weight, measure, money and debit/credit status — and assigned to fixed loci so the fields cannot be confused.

These are all **mechanism precedents**, not claims of derivation. They show that artificial, multi-field, position-sensitive and component-compositional encoding was a live representational technology in the exact northern-Italian period and corridor relevant to the programme.

No candidate yet meets the frozen Grade-A criterion of an extended compositional written notation replacing ordinary prose throughout a technical manuscript. The strongest current candidates are therefore Grade **B+**, not A.

## 2. Frozen grading

- **A**: exact structural precedent — extended compositional notation replaces ordinary prose for technical information.
- **B**: strong mechanism precedent — base sign/component + modifier/context systematically encodes multiple attributes.
- **C**: pedagogical precedent — curriculum/manuscript demonstrably trains formal multidimensional representation but does not itself meet A/B.
- **D**: generic abbreviation/symbolism only.
- **E**: irrelevant resemblance.

No glyph-shape resemblance is evidence.

## 3. Strongest candidate: Matteo da Verona, _De arte memorandi_ — Padua 1420/1423

### 3.1 Provenance and date

The critical-edition literature places composition in Padua in 1420, with a revision in 1423. Matteo was a baccalaureus at the University of Padua by January 1419, lectured there in 1421–22, and became magister in 1422. Multiple manuscript witnesses survive, including Munich BSB Clm 14260, Vat. lat. 6293, Marciana Lat. XIV 292 and others. A St Paul witness has been assigned to the University-of-Padua orbit c. 1435–40.

This is therefore not a later retrospective attribution to Padua: it is a technical treatise produced by a university-trained practitioner in Padua within the radiocarbon-era window.

### 3.2 Artificial composite words

The treatise explicitly instructs the practitioner, when memorising a history or argument, to select key words and form a new word from the **first syllables of three or five selected words**.

Abstract mechanism:

`source word 1 syllable + source word 2 syllable + source word 3 syllable (+ ...) -> artificial composite token`

The important point is structural. A written/spoken surface token need not be lexical in the ordinary sense; it can be mechanically assembled from ordered components whose interpretation is supplied by a learned system.

This is a direct historical precedent for *compositional artificial token formation* in Padua c. 1420.

### 3.3 Multi-field commercial encoding

The same treatise decomposes weights, monetary values, dates and debts into fixed representational fields. Different body positions are assigned different variables; numerical image values are then placed in those fields. The instructions distinguish year, day/month and monetary denominations rather than encoding a record as one undifferentiated image.

Abstract mechanism:

`field identity × locus/position × encoded value -> structured practical record`

This is not ordinary abbreviation. The location of the element is part of its semantics.

### 3.4 Explicit medical state-vector encoding

The closest passage to the frozen structured-notation hypothesis concerns the degrees of natural things according to the primary qualities “as physicians do.” Matteo specifies four quality classes:

- hot + dry;
- hot + moist;
- cold + moist;
- cold + dry.

The classes are assigned distinct columns/attributes. Each column has four vertically ordered positions. Right/left placement distinguishes the two qualities of the pair; vertical position gives degree 1–4. Thus one object can be placed at different right/left heights to encode, for example, a fourth-degree value in one quality and a first-degree value in the other.

Abstract mechanism:

`quality-pair category × side × ordinal position -> graded medical state`

That is an explicit **multidimensional state encoding**, not merely a mnemonic image. It is particularly relevant because the encoded variables are the standard medical primary qualities and their degrees.

### 3.5 Grade

**B+**.

Why not A: the compositional grammar is taught as an artificial-memory system within explanatory prose; it has not yet been shown functioning as a sustained written technical language across an entire working codex.

## 4. Bartolomeo da Mantova, _Liber memoriae artificialis_ — 1429

### 4.1 Fixed four-component artificial codewords

The modern manuscript study describes an explicit procedure at the end of the work: each group of four source figures/items supplies its first syllable; the four syllables are concatenated in order into an artificial four-syllable name. An example reported in the scholarship is:

`TRI + PE + COR + VE -> TRI-PE-COR-VE`

from four named source items.

Abstract mechanism:

`slot 1 syllable + slot 2 syllable + slot 3 syllable + slot 4 syllable -> artificial structured token`

Unlike an ad-hoc abbreviation, this is a reusable rule applied across a prepared inventory.

### 4.2 Other encoded fields

The same system uses formalised images/attributes for numbers, calendar units, dates, debts and credits, including mappings between weekdays and planetary/metals imagery and month-specific symbols. It therefore sits in the same practical computational ecology as Matteo’s field-based systems rather than being only a literary memory trick.

### 4.3 Witnesses and source limitation

The principal illustrated witnesses discussed in modern scholarship include Paris BnF Latin 8684 and BAV Vat. lat. 3677. Direct viewer access was not reliable in this session, so the construction rule is presently grounded in manuscript scholarship rather than our own image-level transcription.

A secondary study states that the method reduces “400 words” to “20 four-syllable codewords.” That numerical statement is **not adopted as verified** here: if four source words generate one codeword, the reported counts are not self-explanatory. The rule itself is clear; the inventory cardinality must be checked against an edition or the manuscript before citation as a quantitative fact.

### 4.4 Grade

**B+**.

Why not A: the artificial words encode a mnemonic apparatus and have not yet been demonstrated as extended running technical notation replacing prose.

## 5. Jacopo Ragona, _Artificialis memoriae regulae_ — 1434, Vicenza/Mantua

Ragona gives an unusually explicit practical example for recording a debt. The information is decomposed into distinct variables including:

- day;
- month;
- year;
- debtor’s name;
- debtor’s father;
- lineage/family;
- weight;
- measure;
- amount of money;
- whether the person owes or is owed.

Different ordered loci/body locations are assigned to the variables so that one field cannot be mistaken for another. Elsewhere the system combines fixed loci with numerical sequences, weekday/planet-metal mappings, and card suit/rank encodings.

Abstract mechanism:

`field position × symbol/image value -> typed record attribute`

A record’s information is therefore distributed across a predetermined representational schema, with position functioning like a field or slot in a data structure.

Grade: **B**.

## 6. Venice, Vat. lat. 10488 — abbacus manuscript, 1424

Ptolemaeus Arabus et Latinus dates and localises Vat. lat. 10488 to Venice in 1424. Scholarship on the manuscript describes several hands working on a common project and identifies algebraic passages that use recurring abbreviations for algebraic species, coefficients, formal fractions, superscripts/spatial disposition and occasional schematic equation-like layouts.

This matters because it is not a treatise about memory: it is an **ordinary practical mathematical manuscript** using compact operational notation in actual computation.

However, the specialist analysis explicitly cautions that the compilers knew symbolic devices but did not use them systematically enough to constitute mature symbolic algebra. That limitation should be preserved, not erased.

Abstract mechanism:

`coefficient + abbreviated species + spatial/relational formatting -> operational expression`

Grade: **B**.

This is currently the strongest candidate for the claim that compact compositional written notation was being used operationally in the Veneto, while the memory treatises are stronger for the claim that artificial multi-slot code construction was consciously taught.

## 7. Paduan student/working-manuscript object class — 1399–1424

The deeper search also changes the evidential basis for the *people and objects* capable of using such systems.

### 7.1 Seville, Biblioteca Capitular y Colombina 7-7-30

PAL identifies ff. 1–109 as copied at Padua by Gilbertus de Olandia, who explicitly describes himself as a medical student at Padua and dates completion to 5 January 1424. The unit contains Pietro d’Abano, natal astrology, material associated with Blasius, sphere/instrument material and computational astronomy.

This is a direct example of a medical student producing a multidisciplinary technical compilation at Padua in the target period.

Grade: **C+** pending folio-level notation inspection.

### 7.2 Student recollectae and working copies around Blasius

Scholarship and manuscript catalogues document students producing _recollectae_ and technical copies in the Paduan arts milieu around 1399–1404, including student transmission of Blasius’s natural-philosophical and mathematical teaching. Earlier identified examples include the 1392 and 1404 copies of _latitudines_ material and student recollections of lecture material.

This is stronger than merely finding a university textbook: the student-generated artefact class itself is attested.

Grade: **C+**.

### 7.3 Medical _latitudo_ as conceptual background

Tiziana Pesenti’s work on late-fourteenth-century Italian _Tegni_ teaching shows that the _latitudo sanitatis_ — graded health, neutrality and disease — was a central problem in commentaries produced at Padua, Perugia and Bologna and often transmitted as students’ recollections. Giovanni Santasofia taught the problem at Padua in the 1370s.

This cannot be treated as a direct source for Matteo’s encoding without transmission evidence. It does, however, independently establish that Paduan medical students were trained to reason in terms of graded bodily states before Matteo’s explicit quality/degree coding system.

Grade: **C contextual support**.

## 8. What the cluster establishes

Taken together, the evidence supports a much stronger historical proposition than the Stage-1 report:

> Between c. 1390 and 1435, the Padua–Veneto–Mantua/Vicenza technical environment contains directly attested systems in which information is decomposed into typed fields, values are encoded by position and ordered degree, artificial words are constructed from fixed component slots, and practical mathematics uses compact operational abbreviations and spatial relations.

The relevant primitives are:

### Artificial-token primitive

`component_1 + component_2 + ... + component_n -> artificial token`

Attested explicitly in Padua 1420 and systematically in Mantua 1429.

### Typed-field primitive

`field/slot identity × value -> record attribute`

Attested in Padua 1420 and Vicenza/Mantua 1434 for dates, money, weights, debts and identities.

### Graded-state primitive

`category × side/dimension × ordinal degree -> multidimensional state`

Attested in Padua 1420 for physicians’ primary qualities.

### Operational-expression primitive

`quantity/coefficient + abbreviated species + spatial relation -> calculation`

Attested in the 1424 Venetian abbacus manuscript.

These primitives are structurally much closer to the VSN-v1 hypothesis `[category/entity] + [operation/property] + [qualifier] + [state]` than generic abbreviation is. But the comparison is still **mechanistic only**. Nothing here identifies a Voynich component, translates a token, establishes a mnemonic purpose, or proves Padua/Veneto origin.

## 9. Why this is still not Grade A

The Grade-A bar should not be moved because the search has become interesting.

The mnemonic systems provide explicit compositional grammars, but they are auxiliary encoding systems explained within ordinary prose rather than a complete running technical script. Vat. lat. 10488 supplies genuine written operational notation, but the specialist literature itself describes the symbolism as incomplete/hesitant rather than a systematic replacement language.

Therefore:

- Matteo: B+;
- Bartolomeo: B+;
- Ragona: B;
- Vat. lat. 10488: B;
- Paduan student/recollecta corpus: C+;
- no A candidate yet.

That negative result is scientifically useful because it defines the remaining target precisely.

## 10. Novelty / rediscovery firewall

The defensible novelty statement is therefore narrow:

## 12. Recommended next Workstream-B experiment

The next justified step is not another broad historical search. It is **mechanism extraction and blinded structural comparison**.

### Phase B2.1 — primary-system extraction

For Matteo, Bartolomeo, Ragona and Vat. lat. 10488, transcribe every recoverable formal encoding rule into a machine-readable grammar before looking at Voynich correspondence. Record:

- number of slots;
- allowed component inventory per slot;
- positional semantics;
- whether order matters;
- whether components fuse phonographically;
- whether a token can omit slots;
- frequency/recurrence of component values;
- context-dependent interpretation;
- whether the same component operates across semantic domains.

### Phase B2.2 — generate the induced surface language

Where the historical grammar is sufficiently explicit, generate the token/string family implied by the system and measure:

- type/token length distributions;
- prefix/suffix entropy;
- positional conditional entropy;
- one-edit minimal-pair graph topology;
- component co-occurrence restrictions;
- family/core structure;
- recurrence and burstiness;
- line/list behaviour where applicable.

### Phase B2.3 — frozen comparison to Voynich

Only after the historical grammars and metrics are frozen, compare them to RF/STA/AAA Voynich using the existing VSN-v1 morphology infrastructure. The purpose is not to tune a generator to Voynich; it is to ask whether **independently attested compositional systems naturally generate the same class of structural invariants**.

A match would strengthen historical/mechanistic plausibility. A mismatch would sharply narrow the structured-notation hypothesis.

## 14. Bottom line

Workstream B is now positive at the **historical-mechanism** level.

The strongest result is not that medieval people used symbols. It is that, in Padua and the immediately connected northern-Italian technical corridor around 1420–1434, we can document explicit procedures for:

- constructing artificial words from ordered syllabic components;
- encoding multidimensional medical qualities by category/side/degree;
- encoding practical records through typed positional fields;
- and writing compact operational mathematical expressions.

That establishes a historically local and chronologically exact precedent for the *technology of compositional encoding*. It does **not** establish that Voynichese is such a system. No Grade-A analogue has yet been found.
