# LAAFU-conditioned E2 v0.2 — results

## Controls

All relevant controls passed after implementation fix 01:
- C0 N0 mean ratio 0.9952; |z|>=2 in 2/20.
- C0 N1 mean ratio 1.0015; |z|>=2 in 2/20.
- C0 N2 mean ratio 0.9968; |z|>=2 in 0/20.
- C0 N3 mean ratio 1.0040; |z|>=2 in 0/20.
- C1 interior-order positive: ratio 1.838, z +14.13.

## ZLZI primary

- N0 line-multiset baseline: E2 ratio **1.2114**, z **+3.88**.
- N1 first2/interior/last2-preserving: **1.0838**, z **+1.68**.
- N2 relative-position-tertile-preserving: **1.1365**, z **+2.82**.
- N3 wholly interior, under N1: **1.0802**, z **+1.13**.

Cross-frame N1: all 12 unique contents were >1, but only 3/12 met ratio>=1.10 and z>=2. Therefore the preregistered H-INLINE criterion was UNSUPPORTED. H-LAAFU was formally NOT SUPPORTED only because N2 narrowly remained above both of its weaker H-LAAFU disappearance thresholds; neither the strong in-line criterion nor N1/N3 survived.

No relative-position hotspot was established under N1: range z -1.29; enrichment fold 1.105.

## Secondary line-length result

The frozen H-LENGTH rule returned SUPPORT at >=17-token loci: that stratum's N3 ratio was 1.426, z +1.85, and it contributed 55.8% of positive observed-minus-null excess; all shorter strata pooled to ratio 1.040, z +0.54.

However, post-result locus inspection showed that the >=17-token stratum was heavily populated by non-paragraph loci. Of 23 interior E2 events on >=17-token ZLZI loci, many occurred on f67–f72 and f86v4 with VTC locus type `Cc`; the IVTFF definition identifies `Cc` as clockwise circular writing. This means the line-length secondary is not a clean test of LAAFU or ordinary paragraph-line structure.

## Consequence

v0.2 is retained as a valid all-locus analysis but is **not the decisive LAAFU test**. The correct next analysis must restrict the primary corpus to standard paragraph loci (`P0`) and separately use all `P*` loci only as a robustness arm. The same frozen null hierarchy is then rerun. No paragraph-only target scores were inspected before that v0.3 protocol was frozen.
