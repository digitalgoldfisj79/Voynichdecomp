# LAAFU-conditioned lag-2 programme v0.2 — preregistration

Frozen: 2026-08-13 before v0.2 statistics were computed.
Branch: `experiment/laafu-conditioned-e2-v0.2-20260813`.

## Question

Does the surviving lag-2 exact recurrence excess represent an additional **within-line ordering constraint**, or can it be absorbed by the already-known fact that the Voynich line is a functional/positional unit (LAAFU)?

This programme therefore does **not** treat a line-start/interior difference as novel. It asks whether E2 remains anomalous after conditioning on line membership **and** coarse within-line position.

Primary observable: `E2 = 1[x_i = x_{i+2}]`. No cross-line pairs. Primary frame ZLZI. Cross-frame replication uses the 12 unique transcription contents frozen in v0.1: GCGA, VDRB-1, TTVE, TTIA, ZLZB, ZLZI, TTLI, VDRB, FFSG, FFSG-2, RGVN, PCCA.

## Null hierarchy

All randomisations preserve line length and exact token multiset.

**N0 — line multiset null (baseline).** Uniformly permute all token positions within each line. This reproduces v0.1 and is reported only as a bridge.

**N1 — boundary-class null.** Within each line, positions are partitioned into FIRST2 = positions 0–1, LAST2 = positions n-2–n-1, and INTERIOR = all remaining positions. Tokens are permuted only inside their original class. For n<5, overlapping boundary membership is resolved deterministically by assigning positions 0–1 to FIRST2 and all remaining positions to LAST2; such short lines contribute to whole-line E2 but not to the confirmatory interior-only statistic.

**N2 — relative-position-tertile null.** Position i in a line of length n is assigned to tertile `min(2, floor(3*i/n))`; tokens are permuted only within the same tertile. This preserves coarse relative-position vocabulary without freezing exact positions.

**N3 — interior-only boundary-conditioned test.** Eligible E2 starts satisfy `i >= 2` and `i+2 <= n-3`, i.e. both endpoints and the intervening token lie outside the first two and last two positions. N3 uses the N1 boundary-class randomisation and scores only these interior triples.

Monte Carlo: 2,000 permutations for ZLZI primary tests; 500 per cross-frame replication test. Seed 20260813. Report observed count/rate, null mean, null SD, ratio and z.

## Known-answer controls

C0 calibration for each null/statistic: create 20 pseudo-corpora by one draw from that null on ZLZI, then analyse each pseudo-corpus against 200 fresh draws from the same null. For N0-E2, N1-E2, N2-E2 and N3-interior-E2, mean ratio must be in [0.95,1.05] and no more than 2/20 replicates may have |z|>=2.

C1 interior-order positive: starting from one N1-shuffled ZLZI pseudo-corpus, choose 2% of non-overlapping N3-eligible starts and set x[i+2]=x[i]. Analyse against N1. N3 E2 must have ratio>=1.15 and z>=3. This establishes that the conditioned null can detect a genuine interior order-2 constraint.

A failed relevant control invalidates the corresponding inference.

## Hypotheses

### H-LAAFU — positional structure is sufficient

Support this statistical sufficiency hypothesis if all three conditioned primary estimates on ZLZI fail to retain a meaningful excess:
- N1 E2: ratio <1.10 OR z<2;
- N2 E2: ratio <1.10 OR z<2;
- N3 interior E2: ratio <1.10 OR z<2.

If supported, the v0.1 E2 residual should be treated as another manifestation of line/position structure unless stronger evidence appears.

### H-INLINE — additional in-line order constraint

Strong support iff, on ZLZI:
- N1 E2 ratio>=1.10 and z>=3;
- N2 E2 ratio>=1.10 and z>=3;
- N3 interior E2 ratio>=1.10 and z>=3;
and under N1 at least 8/12 unique transcription contents have ratio>1.00, with at least 6/12 at ratio>=1.10 and z>=2.

Partial support if ZLZI passes all three but cross-frame replication misses the stated gate, or if exactly two of the three ZLZI conditioned tests pass strongly. Otherwise unsupported.

### H-HOTSPOT — a specific in-line relative-position zone carries the residual

For N3-eligible starts, assign each start to a relative start-position tertile using `min(2,floor(3*i/(n-2)))`. Under N1 permutations compute each tertile's E2 rate and the range `max(rate)-min(rate)`.

Support iff the observed range has permutation z>=3 and the maximum/minimum **enrichment ratios** differ by >=1.25-fold. The selected maximum is not tested separately; the range statistic includes selection in every permutation.

Otherwise no discrete tertile hotspot is established. Report the three enrichment ratios regardless.

### H-LENGTH — line length is the carrier

For N3-eligible lines use frozen length strata: 7–9, 10–12, 13–16, >=17 tokens. Compute N3 E2 under N1 separately by stratum.

Call the effect length-concentrated only if one stratum contributes >50% of the observed-minus-null excess while the pooled remaining strata have ratio<1.05 or |z|<2. Otherwise line length does not provide a simple explanation. This is secondary because power differs by stratum.

## Decision discipline

- A boundary modulation by itself is **not** a new result; LAAFU predicts positional heterogeneity.
- The potentially additional object is E2 surviving N1/N2/N3.
- No semantic, linguistic, cipher, or scribal-production interpretation follows directly.
- No generative model will be fitted in v0.2. A subsequent model is warranted only if H-INLINE survives.
