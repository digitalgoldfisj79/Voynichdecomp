# Paragraph-only LAAFU-conditioned E2 v0.3 — preregistration

Frozen 2026-08-13 before any paragraph-only target score was computed.

## Why v0.3 exists

v0.2 correctly conditioned on line position but included every IVTFF locus. Post-result inspection of its >=17-token secondary showed many carriers were `Cc` loci, i.e. clockwise circular writing, so the all-locus corpus is not a clean test of the already-known **line-as-a-functional-unit (LAAFU)** phenomenon in ordinary running text.

The present run changes only the corpus gate. It asks whether the lag-2 exact recurrence residual survives **inside paragraph text after known positional structure is conditioned away**.

## Corpus gates

Primary corpus: loci whose VTC `u` field has IVTFF subtype exactly `P0` after removing the leading locus-status symbol (`+`, `@`, `=`, `*`, etc.). `P0` is standard paragraph text.

Robustness corpus: all IVTFF paragraph loci whose subtype begins `P` (`P0`, `P1`, `Pb`, `Pc`, `Pr`, `Pt`, etc.). No `L`, `C`, or `R` locus enters either corpus.

Primary transcription frame: ZLZI.
Cross-frame replication: the same 12 unique transcription contents frozen in v0.1/v0.2: GCGA, VDRB-1, TTVE, TTIA, ZLZB, ZLZI, TTLI, VDRB, FFSG, FFSG-2, RGVN, PCCA.

No cross-locus pair is formed. Each P0 locus is treated only according to the token order supplied in the transcription.

## Statistic and null hierarchy

Primary statistic: `E2 = 1[x_i = x_{i+2}]`.

N0: unrestricted within-locus multiset-preserving permutation.

N1: boundary-class-preserving permutation: FIRST2 (positions 0–1), INTERIOR, LAST2 (positions n-2–n-1); tokens permute only within class.

N2: relative-position-tertile-preserving permutation using `min(2,floor(3*i/n))`.

N3: score only wholly interior E2 starts `i>=2` and `i+2<=n-3`, under N1.

The v0.2 Monte Carlo settings are retained: 2,000 permutations for ZLZI primary P0 tests, 500 for cross-frame P0 replication. Seed 20260813.

## Controls

On P0 ZLZI, rerun C0 calibration independently for N0 whole, N1 whole, N2 whole and N3 interior: 20 pseudo-corpora, each analysed against 200 fresh null draws. Pass iff mean ratio in [0.95,1.05] and <=2/20 have |z|>=2.

C1: from an N1-shuffled P0 pseudo-corpus inject non-overlapping exact order-2 closures into 2% of N3-eligible starts. Under N1, N3 must reach ratio>=1.15 and z>=3.

No downstream claim is valid if its relevant control fails.

## Decision hypotheses

### H-P0-LAAFU-SUFFICIENT

Support iff all three P0 conditioned tests fail to retain a meaningful residual:
- N1 whole: ratio<1.10 OR z<2;
- N2 whole: ratio<1.10 OR z<2;
- N3 interior: ratio<1.10 OR z<2.

Interpretation: after restricting to genuine paragraph text and conditioning line/relative position, the original all-locus E2 excess does not require an additional in-line ordering mechanism. This is statistical sufficiency, not a historical causal identification.

### H-P0-INLINE

Strong support iff all three P0 conditioned tests have ratio>=1.10 and z>=3, AND cross-frame P0 N1 replication has >=8/12 ratios>1 and >=6/12 at ratio>=1.10,z>=2.

Partial support iff exactly two of the three ZLZI conditioned tests pass the strong threshold, or all three pass but the cross-frame gate fails.

Otherwise unsupported.

### H-P0-HOTSPOT

Using N3-eligible P0 starts, compare three relative start-position tertiles under N1. Support a discrete hotspot only if the permutation-calibrated range has z>=3 and the maximum/minimum enrichment ratios differ by >=1.25-fold.

### H-P0-LENGTH

Frozen P0 line-length strata: 7–9, 10–12, 13–16, >=17 tokens. Call the residual length-concentrated only if one stratum both (a) contributes >50% of positive observed-minus-null excess and (b) itself has ratio>=1.15 and z>=2, while pooled other strata have ratio<1.05 or |z|<2. This tightens the v0.2 rule so low-power long-locus fluctuations cannot qualify merely because the other bins are null.

## Robustness arm

Repeat ZLZI N0/N1/N2/N3 on all `P*` loci with 1,000 permutations. This arm has no independent success threshold. It is used only to check whether the P0 conclusion reverses when special paragraph subtypes are admitted.

## Interpretation discipline

- LAAFU and the existence of strong line-position effects are prior facts, not discoveries here.
- A P0 boundary or positional gradient is not novel.
- The only potentially additional object is a lag-2 exact recurrence excess that survives P0 restriction plus N1/N2/N3.
- No language/cipher/semantic inference follows directly.
