# Paragraph-only LAAFU-conditioned E2 v0.3 — results

## Corpus

Primary ZLZI P0 corpus: **3,885 loci / 33,200 tokens**. Only IVTFF standard paragraph loci (`P0`) are included. No circular (`C`), radial (`R`) or label (`L`) locus enters the primary analysis.

Robustness all-P corpus: all `P*` paragraph subtypes only.

## Controls

Initial 20-replicate controls:
- C0 N0: mean ratio 0.9932, 0/20 |z|>=2 — PASS.
- C0 N1: mean ratio 0.9923, 1/20 — PASS.
- C0 N2: mean ratio 0.9920, 1/20 — PASS.
- C0 N3: mean ratio 1.0383, 3/20 — **FAIL by one replicate** against the <=2/20 frozen gate.
- C1 injected interior order-2 positive: ratio 1.835, z +13.76 — PASS.

Because the N3 failure was narrow, a separately frozen pseudo-corpus-only calibration addendum was run without changing any target score. Across 100 pseudo-corpora × 500 null draws:
- mean ratio **1.00064**;
- mean z **+0.00697**;
- SD(z) **1.0576**;
- |z|>=2 in **6/100**;
- all four addendum calibration gates PASS.

Thus the original literal v0.3 N3 preregistered control remains recorded as failed, but independent instrument hardening shows N3 is well calibrated and the 3/20 event is consistent with a small-sample fluctuation.

## P0 primary results

| null/test | E2 ratio | z | interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| N0 unrestricted within-P0-line multiset shuffle | **1.2173** | **+3.74** | reproduces baseline excess |
| N1 preserve FIRST2 / INTERIOR / LAST2 | **1.0803** | +1.57 | residual collapses below prereg meaningful threshold |
| N2 preserve relative-position tertile | **1.1380** | +2.63 | moderate residual, below strong in-line threshold |
| N3 wholly interior under N1 | **1.0645** | +0.89 | near null; extended calibration validates instrument |

The key contrast is therefore not "line vs no line". It is that the E2 excess is strong under ordinary within-line shuffling but largely disappears when the known line-boundary positional classes are preserved, and it is absent in the trimmed interior.

## Cross-frame P0 N1 replication

All 12 unique transcription contents have ratio >1, but only **2/12** meet ratio>=1.10 and z>=2. Representative values:

The preregistered H-P0-INLINE mechanism is therefore **UNSUPPORTED**.

## Position and length secondaries

No discrete in-line relative-position hotspot survives N1: hotspot range z -1.40; maximum/minimum enrichment fold 1.059.

## Formal versus scientific conclusion

Literal frozen decisions:
- H-P0-INLINE: **UNSUPPORTED**.
- H-P0-HOTSPOT: **UNSUPPORTED**.
- H-P0-LENGTH: **UNSUPPORTED**.
- H-P0-LAAFU-SUFFICIENT: **NOT SUPPORTED by its deliberately conservative all-three-null criterion**, because N2 remains 1.138 with z +2.63 and because the original N3 20-replicate gate failed.

Scientific synthesis after the independent N3 calibration addendum:

**There is no positive evidence here for an additional paragraph-internal lag-2 ordering mechanism beyond known line-position structure.** The strong N0 excess falls to a weak/non-significant residual when explicit boundary classes are preserved and to near-null in the trimmed interior. The remaining tertile-null residual is weaker and is compatible with incomplete conditioning of the already-known sharp line-boundary positional gradient. This favours LAAFU absorption, but does not meet the deliberately stronger preregistered claim that every positional null makes the residual disappear.
