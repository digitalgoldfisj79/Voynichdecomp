## Why v2.4 is a real additional test

The f.134v–135r primary-source audit exposed a mechanism not covered by terminal Family S:

- Family S had hidden boundaries between variable-length groups of **visible ciphertext symbols**;
- f.134v–135r uses **one visible cipher sign per event**, with variable plaintext expansion to a letter, geminate, CV syllable, null or lexical item.

The exact f.134v–135r syllabary contains 64 one-sign units and was strongly occupied in genuine Paduan controls. B0 therefore passed cleanly; the negative v2.4 result is not an inventory mismatch or a trivial lack-of-data failure.

## Representation conclusions

### Full STA

Full STA is not too small for the historical mechanisms tested. For v2.4, finite Paduan controls activated only 112–124 of the 166 compatible historical slots, and RF top-K occurrence coverage across that range was 0.999657–0.999733. Representation feasibility therefore passed.

### Connected aaa

The official conversion audit found:

- 161 usable full-STA member types;
- 150 distinct connected-aaa signatures;
- eight collision groups;
- top-92 full-STA members collapsing to 88 aaa signatures.

Connected aaa therefore cannot be treated as a lossless relabelling of full STA. Because a full-STA member can expand to multiple aaa units, a raw-aaa historical one-sign model would require a separate segmentation layer. No scientific reason exists to invoke that additional capacity to rescue a model already failing under the richer full-STA representation.

## Historical-source constraint

The primary ledger establishes **available key-sheet assignments**. It does not by itself establish their empirical use frequencies in running ciphertext.

A search of non-Voynich historical material identified real Sforza/Tranchedini encrypted correspondence and modern partial reconstructions, but did not establish a binding obligatory-use rule for the f.134v–135r syllabary. Consequently a new programme may not simply freeze `p_syll>=0.75` because those cells recovered well.

## What remains genuinely open

The closeout is bounded, not metaphysical. A future Tranchedino-related programme is justified only by new evidence of one of the following:

1. a real ciphertext/plaintext or ciphertext/key pairing tied to a specific Cod.2398 system, sufficient to estimate actual class-use rates rather than invent them;
2. a primary-source key with a materially different cipher operation not already represented by alphabetic homophones, nulls, geminates, one-sign syllables, one-sign lexical/nomenclator codes, changing alphabets, or variable visible code groups;
3. an independently justified observable unitisation that adds information rather than merely increasing fitting capacity.

A different choice of optimiser, more restarts, removal of the failing nuisance cells, a free key per section/quire, or relabelling the same carrier with STA/aaa is **not** sufficient reason to reopen the programme.
