# STA within-line / within-zone short-lag discriminator v0.2 — closeout

Date: 2026-08-15
Formal preregistered verdict: **INSTRUMENT_FAIL**.

## Why the formal verdict is INSTRUMENT_FAIL

Source validation, exact line-zone preservation, and the null false-positive calibration all passed.

The failure is entirely the preregistered positive-control power gate:

- 30 line-zone-preserving planted lag-2 corpora;
- frozen q = 0.10;
- median successful within-zone swaps = 32.5;
- median planted lag-2 z = 3.159;
- median planted specificity contrast = 3.053;
- full lag-2-specific gate passed 18/30 = 60%;
- preregistered requirement was >=80%.

Therefore v0.2 cannot use target non-rejection as a formal proof that the null mechanism is sufficient.

The negative calibration was good:

- 200 independent line-zone-null pseudo-observations;
- full-gate false-positive rate = 1/200 = 0.5%;
- median z2 ~ -0.026;
- p99 z2 = 2.472.

The detector is specific but less powerful than preregistered for the planted effect size.

## Target result

The target nevertheless changes the mechanistic picture sharply.

Under the prior exact-length fixed-marginal D2 test:

- observed lag-2 exact matches = 225;
- expected = 125.54;
- ratio = 1.792;
- z = 9.21.

Under the new line-specific START/MID/END inventory null:

- observed = 225;
- expected = 206.303;
- ratio = 1.0906;
- z = 1.610;
- one-sided p = 0.05935;
- four-lag maxT p = 0.21584.

The line-zone inventory null therefore absorbs approximately

`(206.303 - 125.541) / (225 - 125.541) = 0.812`

or **81% of the previous fixed-marginal lag-2 excess**.

## Boundary-side diagnostic

The remaining lag-2 deviation is asymmetric:

- **left boundary:** observed 133, null 109.394, ratio 1.216, z=2.830, p=.00275;
- **right boundary:** observed 127, null 123.635, ratio 1.027, z=.375, p=.371.

This left/right split was secondary and has no rescue authority in v0.2, but it is the clearest surviving structure.

The broad lag-2 phenomenon that motivated the return-mechanism programme is therefore not present at the right edge once each line's own zone inventory is held fixed.

## Revised scientific position

The strongest supported descriptive interpretation is now:

> **Most of the previously striking short-range exact-identity excess is produced by line-local / boundary-zone token-inventory clustering.**

This is much more specific than global section or position effects: individual lines carry restricted local vocabularies, and nearby boundary positions consequently re-use exact token identities even without a special A?A return rule.

The experiment does **not** formally prove that line-zone inventory is the entire mechanism because the positive-control power criterion failed.

It does, however, strongly disfavor the earlier interpretation of a manuscript-wide lag-2 return mechanism:

- whole-boundary lag 2 is not conventionally significant under the line-zone null;
- it fails maxT correction;
- it is not stronger than lag 1;
- its lag-specificity contrast is negative;
- the right boundary residual disappears.

## Only justified follow-up

Do not revive a generic lag-2 copy/return model.

If the programme continues, the remaining target should be the **left boundary only**, because that is where the line-zone null leaves a measurable residual.

A clean next discriminator would test whether the left-edge residual is explained by known exact L0/L1/L2/L3 positional preferences while still conditioning on each line's START/MID inventory. That should be treated as a new, explicitly post-v0.2 question rather than a rescue of the failed primary lag-2 claim.
