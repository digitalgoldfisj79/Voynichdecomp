## 2. Three most damaging problems

**A. It is a group difference, not an order.**
- Inconclusive at this power: with eo777's own text-cluster code added to the null, Path 1 is 0.0960 against 0.1121, null SD 0.0085, ratio 1.89.
- Inside herbal hand 1 alone it is 0.1065 against 0.1243, null SD 0.0157, ratio 1.13.
- What remains is one contrast: cluster 1b sheets are 0.152 log units darker than 1a (null SD 0.050, ratio 3.06, 8 against 10 sheets).
- Paths 2, 3 and 4 give z 3.12, 3.62 and 2.52, so nothing is specific to Path 1.
- The session's check tested only Currier A/B, not his clusters, so "not produced by language" does not cover this.
- Caveat: this null was not on my pre-registered list; it is post-hoc on my side. Power not measured.

**B. "Darkness" is not separable from the edge-softness reading, and the quoted 2.39 under-corrects.**

- Sheet darkness and the focus reading correlate at -0.61.
- The slope is -0.10 within a page, -0.26 over pooled words (what the 2.39 adjustment used) and -0.65 between sheets.
- Inconclusive at this power: adjusted at sheet level, Path 1 is 0.1167 against 0.1205, null SD 0.0086, ratio 0.44.
- I could not show that collapse is specific to focus: random covariates with the same correlation give z 1.69 on average (SD 0.92), and 0.44 sits at their 9th percentile.
- The reading correlates 0.76 between the 2004 and 2014 photographs, so it is mostly a page property, not camera focus. I failed to show a photographic artefact.

**C. Few sheets, a bad stratum and many tests.**
- H:58-65 (quire H) is shuffled against six quire-T sheets. Given its own stratum, z falls to 2.92.
- Dropping the three most influential of 48 sheets gives z 2.00; dropping four gives 1.70.
- The session holds 340 z- or p-bearing results, 44 keyed to Path 1. The permutation p sits at its floor of 1/3001.
- Bonferroni gives 0.015–0.029 over the 44 and 0.11–0.23 over the 340.
- By the log, the order hypothesis's registered primary measure (stroke width) gave z -0.11; darkness was the third measure tried.

## 3. What I could not break

- **Glyph leak:** text-predicted darkness has a neighbour gap of 0.0079, twelve times smaller than the darkness gap (null SD 0.0007, ratio 1.76); removing word identity leaves z 3.59.
- **Layout and parchment:** removing parchment brightness, words per page, token count, coverage or box height leaves z 3.4–3.7.
- **Registered stricter nulls:** within quire 3.59; section × hand × Currier 2.92; section × hand × quire 2.48.

## 4. Probability it survives 60 days (judgment, unverified)

- **Literal statement** (smoother than section × hand chance on Path 1, not on binding): 50%.
- **"This is darkness specifically":** 25%.
- **"Path 1 carries ink order information beyond his text clusters":** 15%.

Against my own pre-registration, the confirm rule (survive all five checks) is not met, and a clean falsification is not achieved either.

## What I am uncertain about

- **Cluster null:** it may over-control. If his clusters are themselves writing batches, conditioning on them removes real signal. I have not planted a known sequence to measure what this null can detect.
- **Focus adjustment:** over-control and confound are not distinguished. The 9th-percentile figure comes from one calibration design.
- **Path 1 against binding:** the two z values were never tested against each other; "smooth on one, not the other" is two separate tests.
- **Not re-derived:** the 2004-photograph replication (z 2.79 in the log), the word-selection step (11,309 of roughly 38,900 words) and the image measurements themselves. I used the supplied word file as given.
- **Duplicates:** 841 rows share a measurement with another row; removing them moved z by 0.06.
- **Access:** nothing was inaccessible. I loaded the repo's phase5.pkl only for the 2004 focus readings.
