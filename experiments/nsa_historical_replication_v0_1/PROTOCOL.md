# NSA Historical Replication Programme v0.1 — Preregistration

## 1. Status and evidence discipline

This document freezes the programme before Voynich outcomes are inspected. Every claim must be labelled **EXACT**, **PUBLISHED**, **MACHINE-CERTIFIED**, **EMPIRICAL**, **HEURISTIC**, or **OPEN**. No failed route may be revived without a new identified ingredient. No manuscript language is drafted from unverified outputs.

The Voynich corpus is the target, not a tuning set. All thresholds, random seeds, panel membership, representations, and promotion rules below are fixed in advance. Exploratory analyses must be written to a separate `exploratory/` namespace and cannot satisfy a primary gate.

## 2. Frozen inputs

- Repository base: `experiment/compression-transfer-v0.1-20260730`.
- Corpus file: repository-root `voynich_transcriptions_slim.json`.
- Loader: repository-root `voynich_slim_loader.py` where compatible; the runner also contains a schema-tolerant parser and records the selected path.
- Primary transliteration: ZLZI.
- Historical panel: exactly the 40 folios in `DATA_PANEL.csv`.
- Historical classes: 12 Biological B, 11 Herbal A, 17 Herbal B.
- Historical sample window: first 400 normalized glyph characters per folio; spaces and line boundaries are excluded from the monographic vector but retained for boundary tests.
- Fixed random seed: 1978.

No folio may be removed because it appears anomalous. Any unavailable or undersized folio is an integrity failure and is reported before analysis.

## 3. Programme stages

### Stage 0 — acquisition and integrity

Required gates:

1. All 40 panel folios resolve uniquely.
2. Each supplies at least 350 normalized glyph characters.
3. The parser preserves folio, line, and token boundaries.
4. SHA-256 hashes are emitted for the corpus, panel, protocol, and runner.
5. A smoke run on synthetic fixtures passes before target execution.

Failure of any gate blocks inferential claims.

### Stage 1 — D’Imperio 40-page replication

Primary representation: normalized monographic frequency vector over the first 400 glyph characters of each folio.

Primary distance: Jensen–Shannon distance with natural logarithms.

Primary tests:

- leave-one-out nearest-centroid classification among the three frozen historical classes;
- within-class versus between-class distance contrast;
- exact-label permutation test with 10,000 fixed-seed permutations;
- anomaly rank within each historical class by leave-one-out centroid distance.

Primary historical anomaly test: folios `f36r`, `f39v`, `f41r`, `f41v`, and `f48v` are evaluated as a frozen five-folio anomaly set. The test asks whether their mean within-labelled-class anomaly percentile exceeds the null mean from all size-five subsets of Herbal B (exact enumeration where feasible).

Promotion gate S1:

- integrity gates pass;
- permutation p ≤ 0.01 for class separation;
- leave-one-out accuracy exceeds the majority baseline by at least 0.20;
- at least three of the frozen five anomalies lie in the top half of Herbal-B anomaly ranks.

The last condition is deliberately weak: the programme tests persistence, not exact recovery of an underspecified 1978 algorithm.

Sensitivity analyses, non-promoting: first 350 characters; full-folio vectors; Hellinger distance; character bigrams; leave-one-folio-out resampling.

### Stage 2 — Currier line and cross-token structure

Primary corpus: all available Biological B lines, with the historical panel reported separately.

Fixed token classes:

- initial class = first normalized glyph;
- terminal class = last normalized glyph;
- line position = first, internal, last, singleton.

Primary statistics:

1. Jensen–Shannon divergence between line-initial and internal token distributions.
2. Mutual information between terminal class of token *t* and initial class of token *t+1* within lines.
3. Boundary discontinuity: observed cross-line continuation score versus 10,000 within-folio line-order-preserving cyclic token-shift nulls.
4. Repeated n-gram boundary crossing count for n=2–5, compared with boundary-shuffled nulls.

Promotion gate S2:

- familywise permutation p ≤ 0.01 after Holm correction across the four primary tests;
- effect direction agrees in at least 80% of Biological-B folios with sufficient data;
- the result survives removal of the five most prolific token types.

### Stage 3 — PTAH-style latent positional structure

This is a modern reconstruction, not a claim to reproduce proprietary PTAH software.

Three frozen representations:

1. glyph stream with explicit token separators;
2. complete tokens;
3. deterministic sub-token chunks generated by all prefixes and suffixes of length 1–3 plus maximal repeated substrings of length 2–4 meeting frequency ≥ 20, learned only on the training folds.

Models: categorical hidden Markov models with K=2…8. Model selection uses training-fold BIC; evaluation uses held-out line log likelihood. Five-state models receive no preference.

Controls:

- token-shuffled Voynich preserving folio token counts;
- within-token glyph-shuffled Voynich preserving token lengths and glyph marginals;
- line-shuffled Voynich preserving tokens;
- natural-language, cipher, codebook, and notation controls already available in the repository, used only when provenance and licensing checks pass.

Promotion gate S3:

- held-out improvement over the best matched null has paired permutation p ≤ 0.01;
- the selected state topology is stable under at least four of five folds (adjusted Rand index ≥ 0.70 after state alignment);
- positional-state ordering replicates independently in two disjoint Biological-B folio halves;
- no claim of syllabary, language, cipher, or notation is permitted from this stage alone.

## 4. Multiplicity and stopping

Stages are hierarchical. Stage 2 and Stage 3 may run after Stage 1 regardless of S1 outcome, but a combined historical-structure claim requires at least two stages to promote. Each stage controls its own primary family at α=0.01. Sensitivity analyses do not rescue a failed primary gate.

The programme stops decisively when:

- all executable stages have passed or failed their frozen gates; or
- a documented data/tooling blocker prevents execution and the repository contains a complete reproducible handover.

## 5. Reporting

Required outputs:

- `INTEGRITY.json`
- `stage1_metrics.json` and `stage1_samples.csv`
- `stage2_metrics.json` when executable
- `stage3_metrics.json` when executable
- `RESULT.md` with exact gate decisions and evidence labels
- machine-readable environment and hashes

A positive result means only that an NSA-era structural observation survives modern controlled reconstruction. It is not a decipherment and does not identify the generating mechanism.
