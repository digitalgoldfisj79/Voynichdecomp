# VBM v12 — Compositional Transducer Synthetic Identifiability Programme

Date: 2026-09-02
Status: **PREREGISTERED BEFORE V12 OUTPUT**
Parent: V11 closeout `V11_MULTIPLE_STRUCTURAL_CONSTRAINTS_JUSTIFY_V12_SYNTHETIC_MODEL`.

## Purpose

V10 showed that arbitrary whole-nucleus and arbitrary whole-bridge lookup dictionaries are non-identifying under language plausibility. V11 independently supported two narrower ciphertext regularities:

1. nuclei sharing an e-skeleton but differing in e-run multiplicity occupy unusually similar boundary contexts (`B_E_LADDER_COMPOSITIONALITY_SUPPORTED`);
2. bridge behaviour is substantially predicted by the two visible halves separately (`C_BRIDGE_HALVES_FACTORISE`).

V12 asks one question only:

> If those two regularities are built into a small explicit generative transducer, do they make the hidden global mapping recoverable from synthetic ciphertext at corpus sizes no larger than the Voynich corpus?

V12 does **not** decode Voynich, does not optimise any language against Voynich, and does not assume the three V11 branches that failed.

## Forbidden assumptions

V12 must not assume:

- exactly five vowel classes;
- that a bridge encodes a vowel at all;
- a direct e-count or e-run -> consonant-run-length rule;
- sequential or cyclic line-edge closure;
- Joachim's Breslau assignments or any plaintext anchors;
- any H1/C1 plaintext material.

The latent symbols below are abstract states, not claimed vowels, consonants, letters, or phonemes.

## Model M12

The factor stream is the same abstract alternation as VBM:

`N0, B0, N1, B1, ... , B(k-1), Nk`

but the hidden values are generated compositionally rather than by independent surface-type dictionaries.

### Nucleus component

Each visible synthetic nucleus type has two known morphological attributes:

- skeleton `s`;
- e-level `m`.

Unknown key parameters are:

- `base[s]` in a latent nucleus alphabet `Z_KN`;
- one global permutation `pi` of `Z_KN`.

The hidden nucleus state is

`N(s,m) = pi^m(base[s])`.

Thus e-level is an operator on a reusable base mapping. It is **not** interpreted as plaintext length.

### Boundary component

Each visible synthetic bridge has known visible halves `(R,L)`.

Unknown key parameters are:

- `u[R]` in `Z_KB`;
- `v[L]` in `Z_KB`.

The hidden bridge state is

`B(R,L) = (u[R] + v[L]) mod KB`.

This is the smallest fixed algebraic factorisation tested here. It is a new restrictive hypothesis motivated by V11-C, not a result already established for Voynich.

Raw `u` and `v` have the expected gauge symmetry `u+c, v-c`; primary recovery therefore scores induced full bridge states. Component recovery is gauge-adjusted.

## Synthetic source

No natural language is used.

For each replicate, latent lines are generated by an alternating Markov source:

- `P(N0)`;
- `P(B | N)`;
- `P(N_next | B)`.

Transition matrices are drawn once from deterministic Dirichlet seeds and then frozen. The exact source matrices are supplied to the solver. This is an intentionally favourable oracle analogous to V10 supplying the correct plaintext language; it removes source-family identification as a failure mode.

Two source families are binding:

- `PEAKED`: Dirichlet concentration 0.25;
- `MODERATE`: Dirichlet concentration 0.75.

Three independent replicates are generated per family (6 total).

Line lengths are deterministic draws from 5..15 nucleus events. A single global key is used for every line in a replicate.

When a latent state is emitted, a compatible surface realisation is sampled from fixed deterministic Dirichlet emission weights. This creates homophones while retaining one true global compositional key.

## Stage A — V11-scale compact surface pressure

Binding dimensions:

- latent nucleus states `KN = 16`;
- latent boundary states `KB = 7`;
- 48 nucleus skeletons;
- e-levels `m in {0,1,2}` -> 144 potential nucleus surface types;
- 10 right-half types;
- 9 left-half types -> 90 potential bridge surface pairs;
- 2,000 total lines per replicate;
- first 80% FIT, final 20% untouched HOLDOUT.

These surface cardinalities deliberately approximate the V11 primary eligible sets (148 nuclei and 89 bridge types) without importing Voynich values or plaintext.

Stage A must pass before Stage B may open.

## Stage B — higher-pressure compositional tail

Opened only if Stage A passes.

Dimensions:

- `KN = 24`;
- `KB = 11`;
- 64 nucleus skeletons;
- e-levels `m in {0,1,2,3}` -> 256 potential nucleus surface types;
- 14 right-half types;
- 13 left-half types -> 182 potential bridge surface pairs;
- 4,000 total lines per replicate;
- first 80% FIT, final 20% untouched HOLDOUT;
- same two source families, three replicates each.

Stage B is a pressure test, not an attempt to reproduce the full Voynich long tail.

## Solver

The solver receives:

- the factor streams and visible morphology `(s,m)` and `(R,L)`;
- `KN`, `KB`;
- the exact generating Markov source matrices;
- the M12 functional form.

It does **not** receive `base`, `pi`, `u`, `v`, induced type maps, HOLDOUT states, or any true-key fragments.

Objective: exact FIT Markov log likelihood, aggregated over surface-type transition counts.

Frozen optimisation:

- 16 deterministic independent starts in Stage A;
- 24 deterministic independent starts in Stage B;
- per start, six coordinate sweeps or convergence, whichever occurs first;
- each sweep conditionally maximises every `base[s]` over all `KN` values;
- conditionally maximises every `u[R]` and `v[L]` over all `KB` values;
- tests all pairwise swaps of `pi` and accepts the best improving swap repeatedly, capped at two complete permutation-swap passes per sweep;
- one final exact coordinate polish;
- retain the highest FIT-likelihood start.

Execution may cache or delta-score likelihoods but may not change candidate moves, starts, sweeps, model dimensions, data, or gates after binding output is exposed.

## Primary recovery metrics

All primary recovery is occurrence-weighted on untouched HOLDOUT.

- `REC_N`: exact induced hidden nucleus-state recovery;
- `REC_B`: exact induced hidden full-bridge-state recovery;
- `REC_ALL`: exact recovery across all latent factor events;
- `REC_N5`, `REC_B5`, `REC_ALL5`: same restricted to surface types seen >=5 times in FIT;
- `COV_N`, `COV_B`: HOLDOUT occurrence coverage by types seen in FIT;
- `HOLD_LM_TRUE`: source log likelihood under the true key;
- `HOLD_LM_FIT`: source log likelihood under recovered key;
- `HOLD_REGRET = HOLD_LM_TRUE - HOLD_LM_FIT` per latent transition;
- `REC_BASE`: skeleton-base recovery;
- `REC_PI`: exact permutation-entry recovery;
- `REC_HALF_GAUGE`: best gauge-adjusted joint recovery of `u` and `v`.

Because component keys can contain representation symmetries, the induced `REC_N`, `REC_B`, and `REC_ALL` metrics are primary; raw component recovery is diagnostic.

## Adversarial controls

At the binding maximum size of each opened stage, generate three matched adversarial architectures per source family using the same latent source, line lengths, surface cardinalities and emission-frequency profile:

- `NUC_BROKEN`: each full `(s,m)` nucleus type has an arbitrary global latent state; bridge remains M12-factorised;
- `BRIDGE_BROKEN`: nucleus remains M12-compositional; each full `(R,L)` pair has an arbitrary global latent state;
- `BOTH_BROKEN`: both full nucleus and full bridge maps are arbitrary.

The M12 solver is fitted unchanged. Negatives have a true full map for oracle likelihood but no meaningful M12 component recovery.

Report `HOLD_REGRET` for each family. A positive model is not considered distinctive if positive regret does not separate from misspecified adversaries.

## Stage A gate

`V12_STAGE_A_IDENTIFIABLE` only if all are true:

1. at least 5/6 positive replicates have `REC_ALL >= 0.90`;
2. at least 5/6 have both `REC_N >= 0.85` and `REC_B >= 0.85`;
3. each source family passes at least 2/3 replicates on conditions 1 and 2;
4. at least 5/6 have `REC_ALL5 >= 0.95`, `REC_N5 >= 0.90`, and `REC_B5 >= 0.90`;
5. median positive `HOLD_REGRET` is lower than the median regret of each of `NUC_BROKEN`, `BRIDGE_BROKEN`, and `BOTH_BROKEN`.

If any conjunct fails:

`V12_COMPOSITIONAL_CONSTRAINTS_NOT_IDENTIFYING_AT_V11_SCALE`

and Stage B is forbidden.

## Stage B gate

If Stage A passes, Stage B qualifies only if all are true at 4,000 lines:

1. at least 5/6 have `REC_ALL >= 0.90`;
2. at least 5/6 have both `REC_N >= 0.85` and `REC_B >= 0.85`;
3. each source family passes at least 2/3;
4. at least 5/6 frequent-only metrics satisfy `.95/.90/.90` as above;
5. positive median regret strictly beats all three Stage-B adversarial medians.

Pass verdict:

`V12_COMPOSITIONAL_TRANSDUCER_IDENTIFIABLE_SYNTHETICALLY`

Failure verdict:

`V12_COMPOSITIONAL_TRANSDUCER_FAILS_PRESSURE_TEST`

## Interpretation and next-step firewall

A V12 pass would establish only that this **new restricted algebraic model** is invertible in synthetic known-source conditions. It would not establish that Voynich uses M12, that bridges are vowels, that e-runs encode consonants, or that any language has been identified.

If and only if Stage B passes, a new V13 may test whether the specific M12 algebraic constraints themselves fit Voynich structure better than matched non-compositional controls, still without plaintext search.

No Voynich decipherment is authorised by V12.

## Evidence rules

- Synthetic replicate/key is the inferential unit.
- No failed gate may be rescued by a new optimiser or altered threshold.
- No post-result change to `KN`, `KB`, component algebra, e-operator form, source concentrations, corpus sizes, or gates.
- Software-only bug fixes are allowed only if no binding scientific result from the affected run was exposed; they must be logged.
- Negative results are committed exactly as positive results are.
