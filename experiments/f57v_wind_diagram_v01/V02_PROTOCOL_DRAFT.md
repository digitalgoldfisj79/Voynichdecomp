# v0.2 protocol draft — historical population test

## Scientific target
Test the *historical availability and persistence* of the f57v-like compound iconographic grammar within medieval wind imagery. Do not attempt to classify f57v by a tuned visual model.

## Corpus
Primary source spine: Thomas Raff's dedicated wind-personification catalogue, supplemented by Barbara Obrist for diagrammatic winds that Raff explicitly does not exhaust. Every retained witness must be tied to a repository/shelfmark/folio (or stable object), date range, and either an authoritative catalogue or a scholarly iconographic identification independent of Voynich literature.

## Frozen feature families
A. circular/concentric framework
B. four principal anthropomorphs
C. axial/cardinal placement of the four
D. extended arms/hands with centre/subordinate relation
E. principal figures holding or controlling subordinate wind heads
F. explicit or unambiguous 4+8=12 hierarchy
G. explicit breath/horn streams
H. discrete central body/emblem

Values: present / absent / unclear. No semantic inference from unread inscriptions is used to convert `unclear` to `present`.

## Endpoints
1. **Existence endpoint:** at least one independently attested medieval wind witness with A+B+C+D and at least one of E/F.
2. **Exact-hierarchy endpoint:** at least one witness with B+E+F.
3. **Chronological-bridge endpoint:** find the latest securely dated witness before 1450 satisfying A+B+C+D; separately latest witness satisfying B+E+F.
4. **Component-continuity endpoint:** identify securely dated >=1350 and >=1400 witnesses retaining at least two of A/B/C/D/E/F/G.
5. **Geographic sensitivity:** identify witnesses from northern Italy / Alpine / south-German regions where provenance is secure.

These are descriptive historical endpoints. No population frequency or p-value will be claimed until Raff's full denominator has been converted into an auditable row-level catalogue.

## Stop rules
- A textual Raff reference without a resolvable object can establish that Raff reports a witness but cannot count as image-verified.
- A later copy of an earlier exemplar is dated as the surviving object, with exemplar dependence recorded separately.
- If exact compound continuity into 1400–1450 cannot be documented, report the gap explicitly rather than interpolating it.
- Voynich-adjacent literature is excluded from source labels and witness selection.

## Current provisional status before full v0.2 enumeration
Existence and exact-hierarchy endpoints already have multiple independent historical witnesses (Heidelberg Cod. Sal. X,16 f.2v; Cambridge Caius 428 f.39v; Marciana lat. 2760 f.2v). The chronological-bridge endpoint remains open and is the main discriminant.
