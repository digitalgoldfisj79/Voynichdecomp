# f57v Wind-Diagram Comparator Programme v0.1

Purpose: test whether Voynich f57v is morphologically closer to medieval wind diagrams than to matched non-wind circular diagrams.

This experiment explicitly separates:
1. family-level iconographic compatibility ("wind-diagram grammar");
2. identification of f57v as a wind diagram;
3. direct exemplar/copying claims (not inferable from this experiment).

Primary analysis is metadata-blind, visual-only, and matched within manuscript where possible. Text semantics, OCR, known wind labels and Voynich-adjacent literature are excluded from the primary endpoint.

See PREREGISTRATION.md and DATA_PANEL.md.
