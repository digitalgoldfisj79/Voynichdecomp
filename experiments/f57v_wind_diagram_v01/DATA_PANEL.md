## Source policy
Primary labels require an authoritative library/museum catalogue, manuscript description, or peer-reviewed scholarly identification tied to a specific manuscript and folio. Search-engine captions, Pinterest, blogs, and Voynich-adjacent literature cannot establish labels.

## Seed corpus
See `source_manifest_seed.csv`. It is a sourcing queue, not the final qualified panel.

Particularly high-value late-medieval seed:
Oxford, Bodleian Library MS. Bodl. 646, c.1460, made at Padua. Saxl/Meier/Bober describe fol. 34r as a table with twelve blowing heads of the winds. Digital Bodleian/Biblissima independently date and localize the manuscript to Padua c.1460. This is temporally and geographically useful, but does not by itself imply any relationship to f57v.

## Acquisition stopping rule
Continue source acquisition until the preregistered minimum panel is met. Do not stop because a favorable or unfavorable f57v result appears.

## Exclusions
- duplicate modern reproductions of the same folio;
- images whose wind label cannot be tied to a manuscript folio;
- post-1600 comparators in the primary panel;
- direct Voynich-derived reconstructions;
- diagrams chosen because they were previously claimed to resemble f57v.
