# f57v inner-ring gesture audit
Date: 2026-08-09
Source: user-supplied colour image of Beinecke MS 408 f57v.

This is a pixels-only descriptive audit. No Voynich transcription or semantic reading is used.

## Stable observations
- Four human heads/busts occupy approximately the four axial/cardinal positions around the central object.
- Each figure is associated with an elongated arm/hand form extending into the interior field.
- The four arm/hand forms are rotationally distributed around the centre rather than being incidental marginal decoration.
- The central object is a discrete six-lobed rosette/cloud-like form with a central dot.
- The entire composition sits inside multiple concentric circular bands with annular writing/marks.

## Gesture morphology
The hands do **not** clearly show four mouths emitting breath. There are no unambiguous wind-stream lines comparable with conventional blowing wind heads.

The arm/hand system nevertheless matters. In the enhanced inner-ring crop:
- upper figure: an arm descends toward the inner-left/lower quadrant and ends in a visible hand;
- right figure: an arm extends inward/down-left and terminates in a visible hand close to the central field;
- lower figure: a long arm extends leftward/upward into the inner field;
- left figure: an arm extends inward/downward into the inner field.

The ensemble gives a rotational, centre-related gesture grammar. Individual fingers are too ambiguous to claim that every hand points to two distinct directions or that any hand definitely holds a small head/ball.

## Comparison discipline
Accordingly, the strongest defensible comparison with Raff/Obrist is **not** 'f57v depicts the exact same held-head device'. It is:

> four axial anthropomorphs + concentric circular organization + extended centre-related arms/hands + a discrete centre, within a medieval tradition in which four principal winds can occupy the axes of a circle and can use their hands to encode subordinate wind heads/directions.

The stronger `held collateral heads` match remains a specific historical precedent and a testable interpretation of the f57v gestures, not an observed fact about f57v.
