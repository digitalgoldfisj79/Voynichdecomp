# VBM v10 — terminal identifiability / phase-transition protocol

Date: 2026-09-01
Branch: `experiment/vbm-v10-terminal-identifiability-20260901`
Status: **FROZEN BEFORE V10 OUTPUT**
Parent: Joachim-exact v9 Q2 closeout `GLOBAL_CODEBOOK_NOT_IDENTIFIABLE_WITH_CURRENT_SOLVER`.

## Purpose

V9 established two things that must not be conflated:

1. fresh line-specific VBM readings are nonselective and receive no evidential weight;
2. the v9 blind solver failed to recover a global VBM codebook even from synthetic data generated by that codebook.

V10 is a final instrument test. It does **not** decode Voynich and it does **not** test German versus another language on Voynich.

Primary question:

> If the plaintext language and admissible VBM value inventory are supplied correctly, can a stronger global optimiser recover a reusable VBM codebook and untouched plaintext at corpus sizes no larger than the usable Voynich corpus?

If not, the current whole-nucleus VBM is treated as practically non-identifiable from ciphertext alone and the decipherment programme stops.

## Fixed VBM architecture

The source-faithful v9/Q0b factor architecture is unchanged:

`N0, B0, N1, B1, ... , B(k-1), Nk`

- bridge surface types have one global value in `{a,e,i,o,u}`;
- non-empty nucleus surface types have one global consonant-run value;
- empty nuclei emit empty;
- homophones are allowed;
- no context-, line-, folio-, hand-, or section-specific values;
- no parser changes;
- no exceptions;
- no word-boundary recovery is attempted.

## Language material

Instrumental plaintext uses deterministic modern `wordfreq` banks for German and Italian. These are not historical-language evidence.

For each language:

- one bank trains a smoothed character 5-gram language model;
- an independently seeded bank supplies synthetic plaintext;
- spaces/punctuation are removed before encoding;
- the admissible nucleus-value inventory is the 32 most frequent non-empty consonant runs of length 1–5 in the language-model bank;
- synthetic plaintext windows are accepted only if every non-empty consonant run is in that frozen 32-run inventory.

The correct language and exact 32-value inventory are given to the solver. V10 therefore deliberately removes language selection as a failure mode.

## Stage A — compact architecture phase transition

This stage asks whether the Q2-style codebook becomes recoverable simply with more evidence and a stronger optimiser.

Per synthetic replicate:

- 30 bridge surface types, exactly six homophones per vowel;
- 96 nucleus surface types, exactly three homophones per each of 32 consonant-run values;
- unequal within-class emission frequencies drawn once per replicate from fixed-seed Dirichlet distributions;
- one global codebook for the whole replicate.

Corpus sizes (total lines):

`100, 250, 500, 1000, 2000`

Each size uses the same deterministic prefix of a 2000-line replicate, so the recovery curve is genuinely cumulative.

At every size:

- first 80% = FIT;
- final 20% = untouched HOLDOUT;
- no HOLDOUT event participates in fitting, restart selection, temperature tuning, or stopping.

Replicates:

- 3 German;
- 3 Italian.

## Stage B — Voynich-tail stress test

Stage B is opened only if Stage A qualifies.

The goal is to introduce the actual identifiability burden visible under the Q0b parser.

The synthetic surface alphabets are fixed to the Q0b TRAIN cardinalities documented before V10:

- 233 bridge surface types;
- 2,715 non-empty nucleus surface types.

Synthetic type-frequency weights are derived mechanically from the empirical Q0b non-firewall Voynich factor counts using the unchanged source-faithful parser. Only **surface frequency ranks/weights** are used; no VBM plaintext/value fit is performed and no H1/C1 plaintext test is opened.

Mapping construction:

- bridge types are assigned round-robin by descending empirical frequency to the five vowel classes, then deterministically permuted within frequency blocks;
- nucleus types are assigned round-robin by descending empirical frequency to the 32 consonant-run classes, then deterministically permuted within frequency blocks;
- when a plaintext value is emitted, a surface homophone is sampled from its class with probability proportional to its empirical Voynich frequency weight plus a fixed pseudocount of 0.25;
- the mapping is global and fixed for the replicate.

This deliberately preserves a heavy surface-type tail similar to the observed parser output while guaranteeing a true global VBM key.

Stage-B corpus sizes:

`500, 1000, 2000, 4000`

Again, each size is a prefix of one 4000-line replicate with an 80/20 FIT/HOLDOUT split.

Replicates:

- 3 German;
- 3 Italian.

The largest 4000-line size is no larger than the documented 4,181-line Q0 TRAIN corpus.

## Stronger global optimiser

The solver is given the correct language and candidate value inventory but not the codebook.

Each fit uses eight deterministic independent chains.

Each chain:

1. frequency-weighted random initial bridge and nucleus assignments;
2. two greedy coordinate-ascent passes over all observed surface types;
3. simulated-annealing proposals over dictionary entries for a frozen number of sweeps;
4. proposal types are:
   - single bridge reassignment;
   - single nucleus reassignment;
   - same-family value swap between two surface types;
5. proposal acceptance is `min(1, exp(delta/T))` on FIT character-5-gram log likelihood;
6. temperature follows a fixed geometric schedule from `T=0.35` to `T=0.01` over 12 sweeps;
7. one final greedy coordinate-ascent polish pass;
8. retain the chain with the highest FIT likelihood.

Efficiency implementation may cache per-line likelihoods and rescore only lines touched by changed surface types. Execution-only optimisations may not alter proposals, temperatures, inventories, data, or gates.

No true-key information is used by the optimiser.

## Oracle ladder

For each positive replicate at each size, report four conditions. Only O0 is trivial; O1–O3 diagnose where identifiability fails.

- `O0_TRUE_KEY`: score the untouched HOLDOUT under the actual key. Sanity check only.
- `O1_25PCT_REVEALED`: reveal the true mappings of the highest-FIT-frequency 25% of bridge types and highest-FIT-frequency 25% of nucleus types; optimise all remaining entries. This is a favourable diagnostic, not the headline test.
- `O2_TRUE_LANGUAGE_ZERO_KEY`: headline blind-key condition. Correct language + exact candidate inventory, zero key values revealed.
- `O3_TRUE_LANGUAGE_FREQUENT_ONLY`: same as O2 but evaluate recovery only on HOLDOUT occurrences whose surface type appeared at least 5 times in FIT. This identifies whether rare-type ambiguity alone explains failure.

## Negative/adversarial controls

At the largest size of each stage, for each language generate:

- `FRESHLINE`: same natural plaintext but a newly permuted codebook on every line;
- `SHUFFLED_GLOBAL`: global-positive factor lines with nucleus positions shuffled among nucleus positions and bridges among bridges within line;
- `MARKOV1`: stable first-order surface Markov process fitted to the corresponding global-positive factor stream.

The solver receives the same correct-language LM and candidate inventory. Negatives have no meaningful true-key recovery statistic.

Report held-out LM advantage over 20 random dictionaries and split-fit map stability. A positive architecture is not considered instrumentally distinctive if its distributions are not above all three adversarial families.

## Primary recovery statistics

Occurrence-weighted on untouched HOLDOUT:

- `REC_B`: exact bridge-value recovery;
- `REC_N`: exact non-empty nucleus-value recovery;
- `REC_CHAR`: exact decoded-character recovery;
- `REC_B5`, `REC_N5`, `REC_CHAR5`: same statistics restricted to surface types seen >=5 times in FIT;
- `COV_B`, `COV_N`: HOLDOUT occurrence coverage by types seen at least once in FIT;
- `HOLD_LM`: fixed language-model log probability per scored character;
- `RAND_HOLD_LM`: median under 20 random dictionaries;
- `HOLD_ADV = HOLD_LM - RAND_HOLD_LM`;
- `STAB`: occurrence-weighted agreement between maps fitted independently on odd/even FIT lines, evaluated on HOLDOUT occurrences.

No crop/event count is treated as an inferential replicate. The replicate is the synthetic corpus/key.

## Frozen success criterion

### Stage A qualification

At some corpus size <=2000 lines, all of the following must hold:

1. at least 5/6 global-positive O2 replicates have `REC_CHAR >= 0.80`;
2. at least 5/6 have `REC_B >= 0.70` **and** `REC_N >= 0.70`;
3. each language passes at least 2/3 replicates on both conditions;
4. at least 5/6 have `REC_CHAR5 >= 0.90` and both `REC_B5, REC_N5 >= 0.80`;
5. at the largest tested size, the minimum positive-family median `HOLD_ADV` and `STAB` each exceed the maximum corresponding adversarial-family median.

If Stage A never satisfies these criteria by 2000 lines, V10 stops as:

`VBM_GLOBAL_KEY_NOT_RECOVERABLE_EVEN_COMPACT`

and Stage B is not opened.

### Stage B terminal qualification

At some corpus size <=4000 lines, all of the following must hold:

1. at least 5/6 O2 replicates have `REC_CHAR >= 0.80`;
2. at least 5/6 have `REC_B >= 0.70` and `REC_N >= 0.70`;
3. each language passes at least 2/3;
4. at least 5/6 have `REC_CHAR5 >= 0.90` and both `REC_B5, REC_N5 >= 0.80`;
5. largest-size positive `HOLD_ADV` and `STAB` family medians strictly separate all binding adversaries.

If Stage A passes but Stage B fails by 4000 lines, close as:

`VBM_FULL_TAIL_NOT_IDENTIFIABLE_AT_VOYNICH_SCALE`

If both stages pass, close as:

`VBM_INSTRUMENT_IDENTIFIABLE_AT_VOYNICH_SCALE`

Only that last verdict permits a future, separately preregistered Voynich TRAIN -> HOLDOUT global-codebook test. It still would not validate VBM or any language.

## Strong negative interpretation

A terminal failure means:

> Under a deliberately favourable known-language, known-value-inventory setting, the present whole-nucleus VBM cannot be reliably inverted at or below the amount of ciphertext available in the Voynich corpus using the frozen strong optimiser.

This is not a mathematical proof of non-identifiability under every possible algorithm. It is a practical stopping result: further claims based on unconstrained Voynich plaintext search have no validated inference instrument.

## Stopping rules

1. No Voynich plaintext decoding in V10.
2. H1/C1 remain unopened.
3. No language search on Voynich.
4. No parser changes.
5. No candidate-inventory expansion after results.
6. No change to corpus sizes, thresholds, temperatures, chain count, or positive/adversarial families after output begins.
7. Stage-B is forbidden if Stage-A fails.
8. Oracle O1 cannot rescue O2; it is diagnostic only.
9. Frequent-only O3 cannot rescue O2; it diagnoses rare-tail failure only.
10. A failed terminal gate remains failed permanently. Any future solver would be a new exploratory research line, not a rescue of V10.
