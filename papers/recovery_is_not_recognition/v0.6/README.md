# Recovery Is Not Recognition v0.6

**Revision date:** 2026-08-03  
**Target:** *Cryptologia*  
**Status:** substantive literature and conceptual reframing complete; ready for external scientific review, subject to independent replication.

Version 0.6 repositions the article within historical decipherment rather than treating the Voynich Manuscript as representative of the field. Accepted script decipherments, successful historical-cipher recoveries, procedural reconstruction, substantial but incomplete recovery, and low-anchor unidentified texts now form the evidential landscape. The experiments are presented as operational stress tests for low-anchor computational inference, not as newly invented standards for a field that lacked validation.

The revision also corrects the treatment of analytical representation. EVA is defined as the **Extensible Voynich Alphabet**, a Voynich-specific scholarly transliteration convention. It is compared functionally with diplomatic and interpretive transcription, EpiDoc practice, GORILA-derived Linear A identifiers, and numbered Indus sign catalogues, without implying that these systems are interchangeable or universal alphabets.
