# Changelog v0.6

## Historical and conceptual reframing

- Replaced the broad implication that unidentified-text research generally neglects validation.
- Established accepted decipherments and recoveries as positive exemplars: Egyptian hieroglyphs, Linear B, Copiale, Mary Stuart's correspondence, and Zodiac 340.
- Added procedural reconstruction through the Royal Game of Ur.
- Added Rohonc as a substantial but incompletely closed recovery case.
- Distinguished accepted decipherment, historical-cipher recovery, procedural reconstruction, partial recovery, low-anchor analysis, and pathological interpretation.
- Defined **low-anchor** as an operational shorthand for cases lacking several external constraints, not as an established historical category.
- Positioned Voynich as a stress case within that landscape rather than as a model of historical decipherment generally.

## Representation and EVA

- Added a dedicated section on transcription, transliteration, numbered sign systems, and editorial representation.
- Defined EVA as the Extensible Voynich Alphabet and documented its Voynich-specific purpose.
- Distinguished convenient transliteration labels from phonetic or linguistic identification.
- Added functional comparators from EpiDoc, GORILA-derived Linear A sign identifiers, and the Mahadevan Indus catalogue.
- Recast representation as part of the measurement model rather than a neutral precondition.

## Evidential positioning

- Recast the five levels as an operationalisation of practices visible in successful decipherment, not a claim to have invented validation standards.
- Added five concrete examples of transitions from bounded computational evidence to stronger interpretation: Hauer–Kondrak, Montemurro–Zanette, Schinner, Timm–Schinner, and the Indus entropy debate.
- Added the pathology-of-cryptology literature while distinguishing mainstream quantitative studies from unconstrained hidden-message extraction.
- Restored a concise many-to-one historical example, the *Sphere of Life and Death*, to motivate recovery of a forward rule and ambiguity class.
- Added compression-method lineage through language-tree and normalized-compression-distance work.

## Design transparency

- Defined **charged** model choices.
- Explained the purpose and coverage logic of the cipher-family portfolio.
- Distinguished calibrated, operational, computational, theoretical, and conventional design choices.
- Added a section roadmap mapping each experiment to the evidential hierarchy.
- Added Supplementary Table S4 documenting principal design choices and decision-rule rationales without pretending that every numerical threshold has a unique theoretical derivation.

## Production

- Expanded the reference list from 38 to 59 entries.
- Rebuilt and visually inspected all DOCX and PDF outputs.
- Retained all experimental values, failed gates, outliers, and non-classification boundaries from v0.5.2.
