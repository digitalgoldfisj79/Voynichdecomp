## Source-to-claim boundaries

1. Chardonnens and Edge support the existence, operation, and manuscript context of the Sphere.
2. The statement “four of seven redactions yield two outcomes” is the project’s exhaustive derived count, not a quotation from those authors.
3. Juste supports difficulty and multiple solutions in Latin transfer; the manuscript does not claim that translation was impossible.
4. Benedetto et al. and Cilibrasi-Vitányi establish the compression-distance literature, not the validity of its application to Voynich.
