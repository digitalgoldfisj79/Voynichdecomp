# Cold Review v0.4

## Verdict

**Substantively stronger; suitable for external review after limited archival work.**

The revision has a clearer central contribution than v0.3. The original manuscript supplied a strong experimental hierarchy but treated “recovery” as if it naturally meant plaintext recovery. The new recovery-object declaration resolves that ambiguity and makes the CoReMA, compression, and historical material part of the same argument rather than appendices to a cipher-versus-generator binary.

## Strongest changes

1. The paper now asks what information a mechanism claims to preserve before selecting an evaluation target.
2. Compression transfer is integrated as a complete negative result, including its decisive shuffled-order control and formal early stop.
3. The onomancy strand supplies a real historical many-to-one system without being misrepresented as cryptography or as a Voynich candidate.
4. The conclusion is broader than cipher classification but remains bounded to cipher-like and structured historical texts.
5. The paper now distinguishes signal existence from signal interpretation across order, proximity, fields, and plaintext.

## Adversarial checks

### Taxonomy inflation

The four regimes could be mistaken for a complete ontology. The manuscript explicitly states that they are operational, non-exhaustive, and potentially hybrid. Acceptable.

### Historical analogy overreach

The greatest temptation is to infer that the Voynich Manuscript may be onomantic. The manuscript explicitly rejects that inference, notes the output-cardinality mismatch, and rejects fitted glyph-value maps. Acceptable.

### Statistical overclaim

The four-of-seven result is an exhaustive count of a closed table. The revised language now states this and attaches no inferential statistic. Acceptable.

### Compression overgeneralisation

The v0.1 failure is bounded to specified general-purpose compressors and representations. The limitations state that it is not a theorem about all coding or learned representations. Acceptable.

### Empirical provenance

All new compression values match the frozen closeout. The executable verifier passes. Acceptable.

### Archival completeness

The source PDF checksum for Chardonnens has not yet been recorded, and the ONOM scripts are not yet in a reviewer-accessible archive. This is the main remaining pre-submission action.

### Manuscript length

The main text is approximately 10.4k words excluding the protocol package. This is longer than v0.3 but the added compression result is substantive. Further shortening should target the early chronological narrative, not the new framework or compression section.

## No-go findings

No fatal mathematical, statistical, or historical inconsistency was identified in the new material. The paper does not claim a Voynich classification, does not reopen a failed gate, and does not use historical analogy as target evidence.
