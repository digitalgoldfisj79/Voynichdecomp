# SG76 — POST-SG75 MECHANISM SURVIVAL TOURNAMENT

## Why this exists
SG47-SG75 repeatedly tested local selectors and state variables. SG76 stops ranking individual statistical models and asks a harder question: which mechanism classes contradict at least one result that has survived calibration and later audits?

No historical prior is used.

Earlier A/B/C calibration selects a true materially coupled shared-state generator 87.04% at rho=.3, 99.38% at rho=.4, and 99.98% at rho=.5. The observed data instead select copy+local-regularisation B. Weakly coupled C remains non-identifiable.

## MECHANISM OUTCOMES

### REJECTED / BOUNDED
- Literal Friderici Class II 35-table mechanism: rejected as whole generator.
- Boundary-tag-only mechanism: rejected as whole generator; retained as mechanism primitive.
- Simple exemplar copying: rejected as sole explanation.
- Literal page-IID nomenclator: rejected unless supplied with a context-sensitive renderer, which changes the model class.
- Tested Markov-only model: disfavoured; not a theorem against arbitrary high-capacity generators.
- Materially coupled shared-state generator: strong form rejected/bounded.

### NOT AN INDEPENDENT EXPLANATION
- Friderici Class IV / pure injective substitution: relabelling only. It preserves source grammar and therefore explains nothing about why the source has the Voynich architecture.

### SURVIVING BROAD ARCHITECTURES
A. SOURCE/CONTENT SEQUENCE + LOCAL STATEFUL SURFACE REGULARISATION
This is the cleanest positive mechanism class currently supported. The earlier calibrated tournament selected its defined B model: BIC -2318.82 versus -2241.26 for simple exemplar and -2312.81 for materially shared-state generation.

B. WEAKLY COUPLED / DECOUPLED AUTONOMOUS GENERATOR + LOCAL SURFACE REGULARISATION
Still alive. If lexical selection and the surface renderer are largely separate, current cross-channel statistics cannot distinguish it from A.

A generalised Friderici-like artificial language/changing-alphabet mechanism can live inside A if it is a renderer of meaningful source text, or inside B if it is autonomous. But it must be non-periodic and effectively open-vocabulary; literal Class II is ruled out.

## CURRENT BOUNDARY

The problem is no longer:
"Is the Voynich random, a simple cipher, or just copied material?"

The current unresolved distinction is:

EXTERNAL/MEANINGFUL SOURCE SEQUENCE
    -> LOCAL STATEFUL RENDERER

versus

AUTONOMOUS LEXICAL SEQUENCE GENERATOR
    -> LARGELY DECOUPLED LOCAL STATEFUL RENDERER

The visible manuscript is compatible with both.

Distinguishing them requires information not supplied by another internal surface classifier: independently anchored content/source identity, paired views/witnesses, an external semantic variable, or a recovery instrument prospectively shown to separate source-bearing renderers from autonomous generation.

## LICENSED INTERPRETATION
Mechanism discrimination has therefore made real progress: the hypothesis space is substantially smaller.

Do not infer plaintext, language, cipher family, semantics, or historical provenance.

Do not state that external source/content is proven. The strongest statement is that source+local-renderer is positively compatible with and supported by the calibrated defined tests, while a weakly coupled autonomous generator remains observationally non-identifiable at current resolution.
