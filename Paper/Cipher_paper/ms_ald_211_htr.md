## NOTES ON THE MANUSCRIPT

### Identification
This is a version of the **Pseudo-Apuleius Herbarius** tradition combined with elements 
possibly from the **Macer Floridus** and vernacular Italian herbal additions. The numbered 
herb entries (S-series, then numbered 49, 70-75, q0-q8) suggest a compiled text drawing 
on multiple sources.

### Language characteristics
- Standard medieval pharmaceutical Latin with strong Italian vernacular influence
- Formula: "Ad [condition]. Accipe [ingredient] de ista herba..."
- Consistent use of "sanabitur/sanatur" outcome formula
- "probatum" (proven) attestation appearing frequently
- Vernacular intrusions: "erba corigola", "lauiztu sento", "zinzibiz" 
- Some entries show Dioscoridean attribution ("Dyascozides dixit")

### Relevance to VMS research
- 14th-century N. Italian pharmaceutical Latin herbal
- Recipe structure: condition → ingredient → preparation → administration → outcome
- Same textual tradition as Circa Instans but from the Pseudo-Apuleius branch
- Herbal illustrations with synonym lists (multilingual plant names)
- Production context: Pavia / N. Italy, parchment, iron-gall ink + rubrication
